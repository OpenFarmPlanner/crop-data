## Short Description
- `Felicia` is a flat-leaf (glatt) leaf-parsley variety, `Petroselinum
  crispum`. Typ: glatt (flat-leaf), an early type within the "Petersilie,
  Blatt" Kultur (see `leaf-parsley.md` for the Typ-vs-Kultur reasoning).
- Dark green, finely cut smooth leaves, described as more aromatic than curly
  types; even development and good regrowth after cutting; tolerant of downy
  mildew
  ([sämereien.ch](https://www.saemereien.ch/petersilie-glatt-felicia-petroselinum-crispum-biosamen),
  [Sativa](https://www.sativa.bio/en/parsley-plain-amsterdamse-snij-felicia-herbs-kr38)).
- Growth duration: reused from the general crop's open guidance (see
  `Comparison With General Crop Data`); no `Felicia`-specific day count to
  first cut was found among the sources checked.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow into a fine, moist seedbed; germination is slow, as
  for leaf parsley generally.
- Sowing: about March/April to early September, in successions (general crop
  guidance; no `Felicia`-specific window found).
- Sowing depth: about 0.5-1.5 cm (general crop guidance).

- Spacing: about 20 cm between rows, 8-10 cm within the row (general crop
  guidance).
- Site: sun to partial shade; humus-rich, evenly moist soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Cut leaves repeatedly once established, leaving the growing point intact;
  good regrowth after cutting is specifically noted for this variety
  ([sämereien.ch](https://www.saemereien.ch/petersilie-glatt-felicia-petroselinum-crispum-biosamen)).
- Fresh use; smooth-leaf types are also easier to clean than curly types.
