## Short Description
- Turmeric (`Kurkuma`, `Curcuma longa`) is a tropical rhizome crop of the
  ginger family (Zingiberaceae), grown in Central Europe in pots, greenhouse,
  or tunnel; separate from `ginger.md` because it has a different species and
  harvest use (yellow-fleshed rhizome).
- Needs even warmer conditions than ginger; typical range 20-30 °C, night
  temperatures should not fall below about 15 °C outdoors
  ([Plantura](https://www.plantura.garden/gemuese/kurkuma/kurkuma-pflanzenportrait)).
- Growth duration: about 9-10 months to yellowing foliage per garden sources;
  left open as planning value.

## Template Adaptation
- Same as ginger (`ginger.md`): rhizome pieces instead of seed. The
  pre-sprouted piece is mapped to the `Transplants` variant; "sowing" means
  starting the pieces. Mapping compromise, flagged in `Research Status`.

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: divide the rhizome into pieces of about 3-5 cm and let the cut
  surfaces dry for a few days; pre-sprout on a heat mat
  ([kraut&rüben](https://www.krautundrueben.de/kurkuma-die-wunderwurzel-anbauen-und-ernten-3398)).
- Start: December to March; shoots appear after about 2-3 weeks. Sources
  differ (December/January vs. early March), see `Research Status`.
- Planting out: after mid-May if grown outdoors in pots; keep warm.
- Planting depth: about 5 cm.

- Spacing: no reliable field spacing found; left open.
- Site: brightest, warmest position, permeable substrate, evenly moist.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest from October, when leaves yellow and die back; alternatively
  overwinter dry at about 10 °C to regrow.
- Used fresh or dried and ground as a spice.

## Notes
