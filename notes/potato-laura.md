## Short Description
- `Laura` is a mid-early ("mittelfrüh"), predominantly firm-cooking
  ("vorwiegend festkochend") potato (`Solanum tuberosum`) with long-oval
  tubers, smooth red skin, and deep-yellow flesh; bred in Germany and
  registered in 1998
  ([Kartoffel-Müller - Laura](https://www.kartoffel-mueller.de/Laura/3018.1)).
- Slightly mealy, creamy flavor; well suited for fries and baked/oven
  potatoes, and also usable for pan-fried potatoes, gratins, and boiled
  potatoes
  ([Kartoffel-Müller - Laura](https://www.kartoffel-mueller.de/Laura/3018.1)).
- Vigorous, strong foliage during cultivation; good storability due to low
  sprouting tendency
  ([Kartoffel-Müller - Laura](https://www.kartoffel-mueller.de/Laura/3018.1)).
- Maturity group: mid-early. Growth-duration sources conflict: one gives
  about 100-120 days to harvest readiness, another gives about 90 days from
  emergence to technical maturity of the tubers. Both are documented rather
  than silently resolved; see `Comparison With General Crop Data`.
- Yield: described qualitatively as medium to high, among the higher-yielding
  potato varieties; no kg/m² figure was found.

## Sowing & Planting

### Seed-Tuber Planting
- Description: plant seed tubers like other mid-early potatoes; no
  `Laura`-specific pre-sprouting or planting-technique guidance beyond the
  general `Kartoffel` note was found.
- Planting: reused from the general `Kartoffel` note (early April to late
  May); no `Laura`-specific planting-date source was found.
- Planting depth: reused from the general `Kartoffel` note (6-10 cm, with a
  10-15 cm alternative documented as an open conflict there); no
  `Laura`-specific figure was found.

- Spacing: reused from the general `Kartoffel` note (about 30-40 cm within
  the row, 60-80 cm between rows); no `Laura`-specific figure was found.
- Site: full sun, loose, well-drained, nutrient-rich soil, matching the
  general `Kartoffel` crop's requirements.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest as a mid-early variety, typically from mid-to-late summer once
  tubers reach technical maturity; exact date not given by the sources
  checked.
- Used as a predominantly firm-cooking potato: fries, oven-baked potatoes,
  pan-fried potatoes, gratins, and boiled potatoes
  ([Kartoffel-Müller - Laura](https://www.kartoffel-mueller.de/Laura/3018.1)).

## Notes
- Crop rotation: avoid growing after other Solanaceae on the same spot for
  several years, as documented in the general `Kartoffel` note.
- Nicknamed the "red queen" among potatoes in some retail sources for its
  red skin and versatility; this is marketing language and is not treated as
  a factual claim.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Maturity group / growth duration: `Laura` is mid-early, which sits within
  the general `Kartoffel` note's overall 90-160 day maturity-group spread,
  consistent with the general note's decision to leave growth duration open.
  `Laura`'s own sources disagree on the specific figure (about 100-120 days
  vs. about 90 days from emergence); this variety note does not pick one, and
  documents both instead, matching the general note's "Model Calendar Values
  Deliberately" approach at the variety level too, because the source basis
  for a single value is not solid enough here.
- Spacing and planting depth: no `Laura`-specific source was found, so the
  general `Kartoffel` note's values are intentionally reused. No
  variety-specific source contradicts them.
- Yield: the general `Kartoffel` note leaves yield open. `Laura` is described
  only qualitatively ("medium to high", "one of the highest-yielding
  varieties") without a kg/m² or kg/plant figure, so no yield value is set
  here either; this is an intentional reuse of the general note's open state,
  not a variety-specific numeric claim.
