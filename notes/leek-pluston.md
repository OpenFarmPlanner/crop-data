## Short Description
- `Pluston F1` is a late-maturing leek hybrid with elongated, medium-thick
  shafts and a high yield reputation
  ([Plantura - Lauchsorten](https://plantura.garden/leserfragen-2/gemuese/lauchsorten-neue-alte-und-robuste-porree-sorten)).
- Positioned by several retail and garden sources as a frost-tolerant, robust
  type that can be harvested through the winter
  ([Gartengemüsekiosk - Winterporree](https://www.gartengemuesekiosk.de/winterporree-anbauen/)
  gives the general winter-leek window; the variety-specific "winter" label
  comes from search-result summaries of shop pages, see `Research Status`).
- Contrast with `Krypton F1` (`leek-krypton.md`): `Krypton` is an early/summer
  type harvested from July; `Pluston` belongs to the late maturity group with
  autumn-to-winter harvest.
- Growth duration: no verifiable variety-specific day count found; left open.

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: sow in trays or a seedbed in spring, transplant into deep
  furrows in early summer, consistent with the general late/winter leek
  pattern in `leek.md`.
- Sowing: April to mid-May (general winter-leek window; no `Pluston`-specific
  sowing date found).
- Outdoor planting: mid-June to end of July (general winter-leek window). A
  commercial listing of `Pluston` young plants suggests availability around
  calendar weeks 22-32 (late May to early August); this is a supply window,
  not an agronomic recommendation.

- Spacing: no `Pluston`-specific figure found. General winter-leek garden
  guidance gives 30-40 cm between rows and 8-15 cm within the row
  ([Gartengemüsekiosk](https://www.gartengemuesekiosk.de/winterporree-anbauen/)),
  which differs from the 50 x 15 cm in `leek.md`; see below.
- Site: full sun, deep fertile soil; last fertilization in August to protect
  winter hardiness (general winter-leek guidance).

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest from October and throughout winter until about April, directly from
  the bed; fleece cover can protect against hard frost and ease harvest in
  snow (general winter-leek guidance).
- Used as a cooked vegetable, as for other leeks.

## Notes
- Identity/type conflict documented explicitly: one source describes `Pluston`
  as an autumn-cultivation variety, others call it a frost-hardy winter leek.
  Both fit the late maturity group; the exact end of the harvest window
  (autumn vs. spring) is not resolved.
- Winter hardiness ratings from breeder or trial data were not found.
