## Short Description
- `Pluston F1` is a late-maturing leek hybrid with elongated, medium-thick
  shafts and a high yield reputation
  ([Plantura - Lauchsorten](https://plantura.garden/leserfragen-2/gemuese/lauchsorten-neue-alte-und-robuste-porree-sorten)).
- Positioned by several retail and garden sources as a frost-tolerant, robust
  type that can be harvested through the winter
  ([Gartengemüsekiosk - Winterporree](https://www.gartengemuesekiosk.de/winterporree-anbauen/)
  gives the general winter-leek window; the variety-specific "winter" label
  comes from search-result summaries of shop pages, see `Research Status`).
- Contrast with `Krypton F1` (`leek-krypton.md`): `Krypton` is an early/summer
  type harvested from July; `Pluston` belongs to the late maturity group with
  autumn-to-winter harvest.
- Growth duration: 120 days from transplanting to the start of harvest
  (inferred; see `Planning Value Derivations`).
- Harvest window: 180 days (October to April).
- Propagation duration: 70 days of pre-cultivation.

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: sow in trays or a seedbed in spring, transplant into deep
  furrows in early summer, consistent with the general late/winter leek
  pattern in `leek.md`.
- Sowing: April to mid-May (general winter-leek window; no `Pluston`-specific
  sowing date found).
- Outdoor planting: mid-June to end of July (general winter-leek window). A
  commercial listing of `Pluston` young plants suggests availability around
  calendar weeks 22-32 (late May to early August); this is a supply window,
  not an agronomic recommendation.

- Spacing: no `Pluston`-specific figure found. General winter-leek garden
  guidance gives 30-40 cm between rows and 8-15 cm within the row
  ([Gartengemüsekiosk](https://www.gartengemuesekiosk.de/winterporree-anbauen/)),
  which differs from the 50 x 15 cm in `leek.md`; see below.
- Site: full sun, deep fertile soil; last fertilization in August to protect
  winter hardiness (general winter-leek guidance).

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest from October and throughout winter until about April, directly from
  the bed; fleece cover can protect against hard frost and ease harvest in
  snow (general winter-leek guidance).
- Used as a cooked vegetable, as for other leeks.

## Notes
- Identity/type conflict documented explicitly: one source describes `Pluston`
  as an autumn-cultivation variety, others call it a frost-hardy winter leek.
  Both fit the late maturity group; the exact end of the harvest window
  (autumn vs. spring) is not resolved.
- Winter hardiness ratings from breeder or trial data were not found.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Maturity group: `Pluston` fits the late/winter group of `leek.md`; this note
  supplies variety-level context but not a source-backed growth duration.
- Sowing and planting timing: matches the general late-type windows (April to
  May sowing, mid-June to July planting); no contradiction found.
- Spacing: general note says about 50 cm x 15 cm; the winter-leek garden
  source says 30-40 cm x 8-15 cm. No variety-specific source; the general
  value is intentionally reused as the planning value and the narrower
  garden figure is recorded as an unresolved source difference.
- Growth duration: general 110 days, `Pluston` 120 days, because late/winter
  types are transplanted mid-June to July and harvested from October. Both
  count from transplanting. No variety-specific day count exists; the value
  is the winter-group value.
- Harvest window: general 90 days, `Pluston` 180 days (October to April, a
  winter-group range, not a variety claim).
- Propagation duration: general 75 days, `Pluston` 70 days (April to mid-May
  sowing, mid-June to July planting).
- Yield: no variety-specific source; "very high yield" is qualitative only
  and stays open.
- Versus `Krypton F1`: later sowing (April-May vs. January), later planting
  (mid-June-July vs. late March-April), harvest October onward vs. July-August.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Definitions: growth duration counts from transplanting; propagation duration
  from sowing to transplanting; harvest window is the harvest period of one
  planting. All values are winter-group inferences, not `Pluston`-specific
  source values.
- Growth duration: 120 days. [Kiepenkerl](https://www.kiepenkerl.de/kulturanleitungen/porree/)
  gives transplanting mid-June to end of July, harvest from October, and
  about 6 months from transplanting for the full winter cycle. Mid-June
  planting to mid-October harvest is about 120 days; late-July plantings need
  about 80-90 days to first harvest. 120 days is used as the value for
  first harvest, with uncertainty of 30 days either way.
- Harvest window: 180 days, October to April (about 6 months), rounded from
  the winter-leek season. A mild-winter or autumn-only harvest would be
  shorter (see the autumn/winter classification conflict).
- Propagation duration: 70 days. Sowing from April to mid-May and transplanting
  from mid-June to July give about 9-10 weeks (63-70 days), matching the
  8-10 weeks in [meine-ernte](https://www.meine-ernte.de/gemuese-abc/porree/)
  (read via a search summary).
- Field/form constraint: whole integer days.
