## Short Description
- `Rocdor` is a yellow wax-podded bush bean, `Phaseolus vulgaris var. nanus
  'Rocdor'`.
- Vigorous, high-yielding plant with fleshy, bright-yellow pods about 16-18 cm
  long and 8-9 mm in diameter; black seed. Bred with resistance to bean common
  mosaic virus (BCMV) and to common bacterial/leaf-spot blight
  ([Hartmann-Brockhaus](https://www.hartmann-brockhaus.de/detail/Buschbohne-Rocdor-gelb---Wachsbohne)).
- Growth duration: 56 days from sowing to first pods, intentionally reused
  from the general crop (no `Rocdor`-specific day count was found; see
  `Comparison With General Crop Data`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow into warm soil after the last frost; like other bush
  beans, `Rocdor` dislikes root disturbance and is not usually pre-cultivated.
- Sowing: about May to June outdoors
  ([Gartencenter Nordharz](https://www.gartencenter-nordharz.de/pflanzen/saatgut/gemuesesamen/bohnensamen/buschbohne-rocdor),
  general bush-bean sources).
- Sowing depth: reused from the general crop; no variety-specific depth was
  found (general guidance for bush beans: about 2-4 cm).

- Spacing: reused from the general crop (about 40 cm between rows); no
  `Rocdor`-specific spacing figure was found.
- Site: full sun, warm, well-drained, nutrient-rich soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Pick pods while young, fleshy, and bright yellow, before seeds swell
  noticeably; repeated picking from around July keeps plants productive.
- Suited for fresh use; wax-podded beans are commonly used fresh or for
  canning/freezing.

- Pod color/use differs qualitatively from the general note's green-pod
  framing: `Rocdor` is a yellow wax bean, a distinct pod-color trait within
  the same `Buschbohne` Kultur; this does not change any structured planning
  field but is noted for clarity.

## Planning Value Derivations

- Growth duration: 56 days from direct sowing to first pods, reused from the
  general crop. No `Rocdor`-specific day count was found. General guidance:
  [Gärtner Pötschke](https://www.poetschke.de/buschbohnen-anbauen-aussaat-pflege-und-ernte/)
  gives about eight weeks (56 days); a general guide summary gives 50-60 days;
  the early green bush bean `Saxa` is quoted at 60-66 days
  ([raimund Biogartenbedarf](https://www.biogartenbedarf.de/bio-saatgut/bohnen-saatgut/buschbohne-saxa/)).
  Inferred, not a variety source value; uncertainty about plus/minus 7 days.
- Harvest window: 11 days, reused from the general crop; both values are
  measured from direct sowing (no propagation). [LWG Veitshöchheim](https://www.lwg.bayern.de/gartenakademie/gartendokumente/infoschriften/064339/index.php)
  gives an average harvest period per sowing of about 8-14 days. Inferred,
  not a `Rocdor` source value.
