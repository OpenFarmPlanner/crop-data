## Short Description
- Shelling pea (`Erbse`, garden types `Markerbse`/marrowfat pea and
  `Palerbse`/round-seeded pea) is `Pisum sativum convar. sativum`, a
  cool-season legume grown for the seed shelled out of the pod, distinct from
  the edible-pod `Zuckererbse` (`sugar-pea.md`, already covered separately).
  Fixes nitrogen, so nutrient demand is low.
- Prefers cool, moist conditions and struggles in summer heat; `Markerbse`
  types are more cold-sensitive at sowing than `Palerbse` types.
- Growth duration: about 73 days from sowing to first pods; harvest window
  about 21 days per sowing (planning values for a mid-season main type, see
  `Planning Value Derivations`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow early; peas dislike transplanting. `Palerbse`
  (round-seeded) types tolerate colder soil and can be sown earlier than
  wrinkle-seeded `Markerbse` types, which need warmer soil to germinate
  reliably.
- Sowing: `Palerbse` from about mid-March; `Markerbse` from about mid-April,
  extending into June for successions
  ([Samen.de - Markerbsen im Jahresverlauf](https://samen.de/blog/markerbsen-im-jahresverlauf-aussaat-und-erntekalender.html)).
- Sowing depth: about 3-5 cm.

- Spacing: rows about 30-40 cm apart (double rows with 60-70 cm gaps between
  pairs also used), 5-10 cm within the row.
- Site: sun to light shade; cool, moisture-retentive, not freshly manured
  soil. Provide support for taller varieties.

## Harvest & Use
- Pick pods once the seeds have filled out but are still tender, roughly 60-70
  days after sowing for early varieties; shell for fresh use, freezing, or
  canning. Regular picking prolongs cropping.

## Notes
- `Markerbse` (marrowfat/wrinkled-seed) types are sweeter than `Palerbse`
  (round-seed) types because of higher sugar content, but are more sensitive
  to cold and wet soil at sowing.
- Susceptible to powdery mildew and Ascochyta/anthracnose leaf spot in humid
  conditions; good air circulation and crop rotation help.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration: 73 days, measured from direct sowing to first pods (no
  propagation; peas are direct sown). Source basis: [Samen.de](https://samen.de/blog/markerbsen-im-jahresverlauf-aussaat-und-erntekalender.html) gives
  55-65 days for early types, 65-80 days for mid-season types and 80-90 days
  for late types. The midpoint of the mid-season range (72.5) is rounded up to
  73 as the representative general value. It fits a mid-season `Markerbse`
  sown in April; early `Palerbse` types are up to about 2 weeks quicker and
  late types (such as `Maxigold`, 85 days) about 2 weeks slower.
- Harvest window: 21 days. The same source reports about 2-3 weeks of
  continuous picking from one sowing when pods are picked every 2-3 days; the
  lower-middle value of 21 days is used. Season-long harvest comes from
  successive sowings, not from a longer window per sowing.
- Yield: 0.8 kg/m² (`per_sqm`), used as a mid-season general planning value.
  Source basis: a 2024 variety-trial series at the Saxon state institute
  ([gartenbau.sachsen.de - Markerbsen](https://www.gartenbau.sachsen.de/markerbsen-41106.html))
  reports kg/m² yields (on a TW120 tenderometer basis) of 0.28-0.80 kg/m² for
  early types, over 1 kg/m² for the best mid-early types, 0.39-0.94 kg/m² for
  mid-late types and 0.35-0.72 kg/m² for late types; a garden-yield reference
  table on [forum.garten-pur.de](https://forum.garten-pur.de/index.php?topic=28046.0)
  separately gives "Erbsen, grüne Pflück- 0,5-1,2" kg/m². Derivation: these
  ranges overlap around 0.5-1.0 kg/m²; 0.8 kg/m² is used as a representative
  mid-season planning value. Uncertainty: early types sit lower (about
  0.3-0.5 kg/m²) and high-yielding marrowfat/late types can reach 1.0 kg/m²
  or more (see `shelling-pea-maxigold.md`).
- Thousand kernel weight: left open; large-seeded marrowfat varieties (see
  `shelling-pea-maxigold.md`) run well above typical round-seeded garden pea
  weights, so no single general figure is set without variety differentiation.
  Re-checked 2026-09-22: a marrowfat reference variety, `Wunder von Kelvedon`,
  is listed at 217.37 g per thousand seeds
  ([raimund Biogartenbedarf](https://www.biogartenbedarf.de/bio-saatgut/erbsen-saatgut/markerbse-wunder-von-kelvedon/)),
  while general agronomic sources note pea thousand-kernel weight can fall
  under 200 g for some types. This additional data point still spans a wide
  range and does not resolve the open question for a mixed `Markerbse`/
  `Palerbse` general crop; it is documented here rather than used to set a
  falsely precise general figure.
