## Short Description
- `Zierkürbis` (ornamental pumpkin/gourd) is grown for decoration and, in the
  carving type, for Halloween lanterns rather than primarily for the kitchen.
  ReinSaat lists a separate category "Ornamental pumpkins" with entries such
  as Goblin Eggs, an ornamental gourd mix, Speckled Swan and Kronenkürbis
  ([ReinSaat - Ornamental pumpkins](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/zierkuerbis/)).
- Carving type (`Schnitzkürbis`, "Jack o'Lantern"): described by
  [ReinSaat](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/zierkuerbis/jack_o_lantern/)
  as a Cucurbita pepo type with fruits of about 3 kg and orange-gold flesh,
  storable roughly 4 months. No specific variety is maintained here; this is a
  type note.
- Identity issue documented explicitly: ReinSaat lists `Jack o'Lantern` under
  both the ornamental and the edible category
  ([ReinSaat edible listing](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/jack_o_lantern/)).
  This note keeps ornamental/carving pumpkins separate from `pumpkin.md`
  (edible `Kürbis`: Hokkaido = C. maxima, Butternut = C. moschata). Species
  overlap: ornamental types are mostly C. pepo but may include other species
  (a gourd mix is not species-resolved), so the Kultur is defined by use, not
  species. Whether it should be one Kultur or split (carving pumpkins vs.
  small ornamental gourds) is an open mapping question.
- Growth duration and harvest window: left open at general level; ornamental
  types differ, and no source gives day counts.

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate in pots indoors or in a warm greenhouse from
  late April to May (a retailer summary mentions March), transplant after the
  last frosts.
- Sowing: late April to May.
- Outdoor planting: after mid-May.

### Direct Sowing
- Description: from mid-May when the soil is at least 10-12 °C.
- Sowing: mid-May onward.

- Spacing: about 2 m between rows and 1 m within the row for the carving
  type (ReinSaat); other ornamental types not sourced.
- Site: warm, sunny, well-fertilized, cold- and moisture-sensitive.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest September to October when fully ripe.
- Decoration and carving; flesh of the carving type is edible but the general
  ornamental-gourd flesh may be bitter; not verified here.

## Notes
- Yield: no kg/m² source; fruit weight (about 3 kg) is per fruit. A per-plant
  count is not sourced, so no conversion was made. Left open.
- Rotation: as for other Cucurbitaceae, see `pumpkin.md`.
- Sowing depth not sourced; `pumpkin.md` gives 2-3 cm for edible pumpkins and
  is not silently reused.
