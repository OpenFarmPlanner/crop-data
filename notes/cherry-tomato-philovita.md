## Short Description
- `Philovita` (F1) is described as the first cherry tomato variety with
  resistance to late blight (Phytophthora infestans), alongside resistance
  to leaf mold, fusarium wilt, and tomato mosaic virus
  ([Tomaten.de](https://www.tomaten.de/tomate-philovita-cherrytomate/)).
  Confirmed as a `Kirschtomate` via [ReinSaat](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/tomaten_paradeiser/),
  which lists cherry tomatoes as their own subcategory.
- Fruit weight: about 15 g; deep-red, round fruit on long trusses of up to
  70 fruits.
- Flavor described as fruity and sweet-aromatic with slight acidity.
- Bred by De Ruiter Seeds (Bayer/Monsanto group); F1 hybrid, not seed-stable.
- Plant grows relatively slowly but can still reach up to 2 m tall.
- Growth duration: 65 days from transplanting; harvest window 80 days;
  propagation duration 49 days. No variety-specific figure was found, so the
  general `Kirschtomate` planning values are intentionally reused (see `Comparison
  With General Crop Data`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: Sow in trays indoors. Plant outdoors after mid-May with a
  support structure/stake.
- Sowing: 02-01 to 04-30
- Outdoor planting: from mid-May

- Spacing: 80 cm between rows; 50 cm within the row (the more frequently
  reported figure across sources; one source instead gives a minimum of
  60 cm in-row and 80 cm row spacing - see open source conflict below)
- Site: full sun, warm and sheltered; deep, humus-rich, evenly moist soil

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest by the truss once fruit is deep red; long trusses (up to 70
  fruits) allow repeated picking over an extended season.
- Eaten fresh, whole; high sugar content makes it suited to snacking and
  salads.

## Notes
- Notably resistant to late blight, which is unusual for a cherry tomato
  and can reduce fungicide need in wet seasons.
- As an F1 hybrid, seed cannot be saved true-to-type.

- Uncertainty: fits a staked crop planted out in mid-May; protected
