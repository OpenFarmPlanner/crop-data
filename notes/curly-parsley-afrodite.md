## Short Description
- `Afrodite` (also spelled `Aphrodite`) is a parsley variety, `Petroselinum
  crispum`. Kultur: Krause Petersilie (English name Curly parsley); type
  curly, assigned provisionally with medium confidence (see "Identity And
  Mapping Note"). See `curly-parsley.md`.
- Dark green, aromatic foliage with short, strong stems; described as suited
  to pots and balcony boxes and mostly used fresh
  ([Garten Schlüter](https://www.garten-schlueter.de/saatgut/kraeuter-saatgut/petersilie/petersilie-aromapetersilie-afrodite)).
- Growth duration: 90 days from direct sowing to first cut; harvest window 84
  days for repeated cuts. Both are the values of the general curly-parsley
  note, intentionally reused. See Planning Value Derivations.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow into a fine, moist seedbed; suitable for field rows
  and containers.
- Sowing: 2026-04-01 to 2026-07-15 (general curly-parsley window, reused).
- Sowing depth: 1.5 cm (1-2 cm), general curly-parsley value.

- Spacing: 25 cm between rows; 3 cm within the row (general commercial
  spacing, reused).
- Site: sun to partial shade; humus-rich, evenly moist soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Cut leaves repeatedly once established, leaving the growing point intact.
- Best used fresh; aroma diminishes on drying.

## Notes
- Some retailers spell the name "Aphrodite"; the two spellings are treated as
  one variety because titles and descriptions match. Documented explicitly
  rather than silently choosing one as authoritative.

## Identity And Mapping Note
- Decision 2026-09-25: assigned to Krause Petersilie with medium confidence,
  provisional.
- Evidence for curly: retailer titles call it "Krause Petersilie 'Aphrodite'"
  ([Floragard](https://www.floragard.de/de-de/pflanzeninfothek/pflanze/kraeuter/krause-petersilie-aphrodite),
  [Mauk Gartenwelt](https://www.mauk-gartenwelt.de/petroselinum-crispum-aphrodite-krause-petersilie-aphrodite),
  [GartenBaumschule Becker](https://www.gartenbaumschule-becker.de/artikel/57184/krause-petersilie-aphrodite));
  search summaries of the Kiepenkerl description mention densely curled leaves
  and a particularly aromatic moss-curled type.
- Limits: the supplier pages that could be opened
  ([Garten Schlüter](https://www.garten-schlueter.de/saatgut/kraeuter-saatgut/petersilie/petersilie-aromapetersilie-afrodite),
  Samenhaus for the Kiepenkerl product) describe dark green, aromatic foliage
  with short strong stems but do not name the leaf shape. The breeder text
  could not be read (Kiepenkerl page returned HTTP 404). No source says it is
  smooth. The retailer URLs for Floragard and Mauk are site roots, not
  verified product pages; recheck before publishing.
- Spelling: Afrodite versus Aphrodite, see Notes.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration and harvest window: reused from `curly-parsley.md`, where
  they derive from the [DLR Rheinpfalz sheet](https://www.bbs-landwirtschaft.de/Internet/global/themen.nsf/0/1ef319fcf05534dbc125876e002d5a6f/$FILE/Petersilie,%20Blatt.pdf)
  (80 days reference plus about 7 days for curly types, rounded to 90; three
  cuts at 6-week steps = 84 days). Not presented as `Afrodite`-specific
  claims. Uncertainty about +/- 10 days.
- All other values: reused unchanged; see the general note's derivations.
