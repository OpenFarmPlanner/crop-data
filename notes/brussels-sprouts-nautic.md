## Short Description
- `Nautic` (`Nautic F1`) is an early-to-mid-early Brussels sprouts F1 hybrid,
  `Brassica oleracea var. gemmifera`, bred for organic cultivation, matched to
  the general crop note `notes/brussels-sprouts.md`.
- Round, fine sprouts with dark green color and a fine stem attachment; good
  field durability. Described by breeders/retailers as having good resistance
  to white rust (`Weißer Rost`), powdery mildew, and `Mycosphaerella` leaf
  spot.
- Growth duration: about 155 days as a variety-specific planning value (see
  `Planning Value Derivations`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate for transplanting; typical planting period for
  this variety runs from early to late May.
- Sowing: from about March under protection (general crop guidance; no
  variety-specific sowing-date source found).
- Outdoor planting: early to late May.

- Spacing: no variety-specific spacing source found; the general crop's range
  (about 50-60 cm between rows, 50-60 cm within the row) is reused (see
  `Comparison With General Crop Data`).
- Site: full sun; firm, nutrient-rich, well-drained soil with steady moisture.
  High nutrient demand.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest from early October to mid-November, progressively from the bottom of
  the stem upward.
- Eaten cooked.
