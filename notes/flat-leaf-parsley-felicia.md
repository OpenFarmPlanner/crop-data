## Short Description
- `Felicia` is a flat-leaf (glatt) parsley variety, `Petroselinum crispum`.
  Kultur: Glatte Petersilie (English name Flat-leaf parsley); type flat-leaf,
  confirmed by two seed suppliers ([Sativa](https://www.sativa.bio/en/parsley-plain-amsterdamse-snij-felicia-herbs-kr38)
  and [sämereien.ch](https://www.saemereien.ch/petersilie-glatt-felicia-petroselinum-crispum-biosamen)).
  An early type. See `flat-leaf-parsley.md`.
- Dark green, finely cut smooth leaves, described as more aromatic than curly
  types; even development and good regrowth after cutting; tolerant of downy
  mildew (same two suppliers).
- Growth duration: 85 days from direct sowing to first cut; harvest window 70
  days. Both are the values of the general flat-leaf note, intentionally
  reused (see Comparison and the open conflict below).

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow into a fine, moist seedbed; germination is slow, as
  for parsley generally.
- Sowing: 2026-03-01 to 2026-07-31 (general flat-leaf window, reused; no
  `Felicia`-specific window found).
- Sowing depth: 1.5 cm (1-2 cm), general value.

- Spacing: 25 cm between rows; 3 cm within the row (general commercial spacing,
  reused).
- Site: sun to partial shade; humus-rich, evenly moist soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Cut leaves repeatedly once established, leaving the growing point intact;
  good regrowth after cutting is noted for this variety by
  [sämereien.ch](https://www.saemereien.ch/petersilie-glatt-felicia-petroselinum-crispum-biosamen).
- Fresh use; smooth-leaf types are also easier to clean than curly types.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
General crop note: `flat-leaf-parsley.md` (Kultur Glatte Petersilie). The
earlier note reused values of the former mixed Kultur; they are replaced by the
matching values of the new note.
- Type: flat-leaf, matches the Kultur; confirmed by two suppliers.
- Growth duration: 85 days, intentional reuse of the general value (previous
  90 days). Conflict: the earlier note read the DLR table as stated for
  `Felicia` (92/91/80/92 days, mean about 89), while the general note records
  that the table refers to a curly reference variety with +7 days for curly
  types. Which reading is right is unresolved, so no variety-sourced value is
  claimed; the possible deviation (about 90 versus 85 days) stays an open
  question.
- Harvest window: 70 days, intentional reuse (previous 105 days assumed four
  cuts). No deviation claimed.
- Propagation (45 days), sowing windows, sowing depth, spacing (25 x 3 cm):
  no `Felicia` data; intentional reuse.
- Yield: 6 kg/m² per season. No reliable variety-specific yield source found;
  the general crop planning value was intentionally reused. The DLR yield
  figures are not variety-specific.
- Thousand kernel weight: 1.5 g, intentional reuse.
- Nutrient demand: high, intentional reuse (previous "medium" came from the
  old mixed note).
- Frost and overwintering: no variety data; general note applies. Do not assume
  overwintering suitability; the DLR variety table was not read for `Felicia`.
- Variety-sourced: only the type, leaf description, aroma, regrowth and
  downy-mildew tolerance.

## Planning Value Derivations
- All planning values are reused from `flat-leaf-parsley.md` (85 days growth
  duration, 70 days harvest window = first cut plus two regrowth cuts at
  35 days, 6 kg/m² from three cuts, 1.5 g TKW, high nutrient demand); see
  its derivations, based on the [DLR Rheinpfalz sheet](https://www.bbs-landwirtschaft.de/Internet/global/themen.nsf/0/1ef319fcf05534dbc125876e002d5a6f/$FILE/Petersilie,%20Blatt.pdf).
