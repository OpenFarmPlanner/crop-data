## Short Description
- Stalk celery (`Staudensellerie`, also `Stangensellerie`) is `Apium graveolens
  var. dulce`, grown for its fleshy leaf stalks. It is the same species as
  celeriac (`Knollensellerie`, see `celeriac.md`) but deliberately a separate
  crop (Kultur) because harvest form (stalks, cut in summer to autumn) and
  crop calendar differ. Leaf/cutting celery is not covered.
- Warm-season, frost-sensitive, heavy water and nutrient demand; high boron
  demand (heart rot risk)
  ([Hortipendium](https://www.hortipendium.de/Stangensellerie)). Modern
  practice favors green-stemmed types; older self-blanching/earthed-up types
  need blanching.
- Growth duration: left open for the general crop; early and late types
  differ (e.g. about 75 days from planting for `Conga` vs about 80 days and a
  longer total culture for `Tango`), and sources give about 80 days as a
  generic figure. See variety notes.

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate (light germinator, about 8 weeks from sowing to
  planting per garden sources); plant out only after frost risk has passed.
- Sowing: about February to mid-May.
- Outdoor planting: about 8 weeks after sowing; planting from about April to
  early/mid-July (Hortipendium: mid-March to mid-July depending on region).

- Spacing: about 40 x 40 cm in garden sources; commercial guidance stresses
  adapting row spacing to machinery and prefers dense in-row spacing.
- Site: sun, deep humus-rich, constantly moist soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Cut whole plants or outer stalks once stalks are full length; harvest from
  about June to October depending on type and planting date.
- Fresh, cooked, juice; leaves as seasoning.

## Notes
- Rotation: 3-4 years before Apiaceae again.

## Identity And Mapping Note
- Celery vs celeriac: same species, separated deliberately (harvest form and
  calendar). Bundle celery (`Bundsellerie`) is a distinct commercial form and
  not covered.
- Blanching vs green types: treated as a variety trait, not a Kultur split;
  revisit if blanching changes the calendar significantly.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration: left open; from-planting figures of about 75-80 days depend
  on type, and total culture from sowing is about 150-170 days for late types.
- Harvest window: left open; no reliable general figure.
- Yield: left open; no reliable kg/m² source was found.
- Spacing: 0.40 x 0.40 m is documented as a garden value only.
- Thousand kernel weight: left open; a variety supplier gives about 370 seeds
  per 0.1 g, i.e. roughly 0.27 g, which is variety/lot specific.
