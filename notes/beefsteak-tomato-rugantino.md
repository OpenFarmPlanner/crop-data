## Short Description
- `Rugantino` (RZ F1) is an early-ripening, high-yielding oxheart-type
  ("Coeur de Boeuf"/Ochsenherz) beefsteak tomato with a heart-shaped fruit
  and a broad disease-resistance package for field and greenhouse
  cultivation ([Dürr Samen](https://shop.duerr-samen.de/ochsenherz-tomaten/ochsenherz-tomaten-rugantino-rz-f1.html)).
  Confirmed as a `Fleischtomate` (beefsteak) via [ReinSaat](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/tomaten_paradeiser/),
  which files "Ochsenherz Cuor di bue" varieties under Beef tomatoes.
- Fruit weight: about 180-220 g.
- F1 hybrid; not seed-stable, so seed must be re-bought each season rather
  than saved.
- Growth duration: 75 days from transplanting; harvest window 70 days;
  propagation duration 49 days. No variety-specific figure was found, so the
  general `Fleischtomate` planning values are intentionally reused (see `Comparison
  With General Crop Data`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: Sow in trays indoors, cover seed only lightly. Germinates
  from about 18 °C in 8-10 days, optimal 20-22 °C.
- Sowing: 02-01 to 04-01
- Sowing depth: about 0.5 cm (reused from the general `Fleischtomate` note;
  the source describes covering the seed only lightly, consistent with this
  figure).
- Outdoor planting: from mid-May, staked/trellised

- Spacing: 80 cm between rows; 50 cm within the row
  ([Dürr Samen](https://shop.duerr-samen.de/ochsenherz-tomaten/ochsenherz-tomaten-rugantino-rz-f1.html))
- Site: full sun, warm and sheltered; deep, humus-rich, evenly moist soil

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest when fruit is fully red and the heart shape has filled out.
- Fresh eating and slicing; low-seed, meaty flesh typical of the oxheart
  type; strongly aromatic flavor is highlighted by the source.

## Notes
- Bred for field and greenhouse use with a resistance package (exact
  pathogen list not published on the retail pages reviewed).
- As an F1 hybrid, expect uniform fruit but no true-to-type seed saving.
