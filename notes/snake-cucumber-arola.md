## Short Description
- `Arola` is a `Schlangengurke` (`Cucumis sativus`), confirmed via
  [ReinSaat - Gurken > Schlangengurken](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/gurken/schlangengurken/).
  `Gurke` is used here only as a generic alias, not as the Kultur name.
- Mixed-flowering variety recommended for protected/greenhouse cultivation
  ("geschützte Kultur empfohlen"), though it is also grown in the open field
  ([ReinSaat - Arola](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/gurken/salatgurken/arola/)).
- Long, medium to dark green, slightly spiny fruits with a firmer skin than
  typical for the type (edible unpeeled); fresh, aromatic flavor. Very robust,
  resistant to common pests and diseases, and can be harvested into autumn.
  Fruits form mainly on the side shoots, so side shoots should be cut only
  after fruiting rather than removed early
  ([ReinSaat - Arola](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/gurken/salatgurken/arola/)).
- Growth duration: not given as a day-count; reused (left open) from the
  general `Schlangengurke` crop (see `Comparison With General Crop Data`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate in a greenhouse from early March, or in the
  field from mid-April; keep warm during germination (ideal 25-28 °C, cooler
  after sprouting).
- Sowing: early March (greenhouse pre-cultivation) to mid-April (field
  pre-cultivation)
  ([ReinSaat - Arola](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/gurken/salatgurken/arola/)).
- Outdoor/greenhouse planting: mid-April (greenhouse) to mid-May (field)
  ([ReinSaat - Arola](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/gurken/salatgurken/arola/)).

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow once soil has warmed; avoid waterlogging.
- Sowing: from mid-May
  ([ReinSaat - Arola](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/gurken/salatgurken/arola/)).
- Sowing depth: about 2 cm
  ([ReinSaat - Arola](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/gurken/salatgurken/arola/)).

- Spacing: 100 x 40 cm in the greenhouse; 100 x 30-40 cm outdoors
  ([ReinSaat - Arola](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/gurken/salatgurken/arola/)).
- Site: full sun, warm; greenhouse/protected cultivation is explicitly
  recommended by the source for best results, though open-field cultivation is
  also supported.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest continuously; the variety's robustness against pests and diseases
  allows harvest into autumn, later than many other snake cucumber varieties.
- Used fresh; skin is firmer than typical but described as pleasant to eat
  unpeeled.

## Notes
- Side-shoot management: because fruit forms mainly on side shoots, do not
  remove them preemptively; only trim them after fruit has set, unlike
  varieties where side shoots are pinched early.
- Thousand seed mass: 27.55 g
  ([ReinSaat - Arola](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/gurken/salatgurken/arola/)).

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Spacing (100 x 40 cm greenhouse, 100 x 30-40 cm outdoors) matches `Tanja`'s
  variety-sourced spacing almost exactly and sits within the general crop's
  disputed range; kept as the more specific variety-sourced value.
- Sowing depth (about 2 cm) matches the general crop's 2-3 cm guidance.
- Growth duration and harvest window: no `Arola`-specific day-count was
  found. Left open, consistent with the general `Schlangengurke` note, which
  also leaves them open. This is an intentional non-decision at both levels,
  not a reuse of a general planning value.
- Yield: no kg/m² or piece-count figure given by the source beyond the
  qualitative "harvestable into autumn" claim; left open, same handling as
  `Tanja`.
- Thousand seed mass (27.55 g) is close to `Tanja`'s stated 15-30 g range but
  is documented separately since it comes from a distinct source figure, not
  a general-crop value.
- Cultivation recommendation differs slightly from `Tanja`: `Arola`'s source
  explicitly recommends protected/greenhouse cultivation, while `Tanja`'s
  source presents open-field and cold-greenhouse cultivation as equally
  standard. Both are still supported for open-field growing per their
  respective sources, so this is noted as a variety nuance, not a Kultur-level
  distinction.
