## Short Description
- `Hokkaido` (also called `Zwiebelkürbis`/`Uchiki Kuri`) is `Cucurbita
  maxima`, confirmed via
  [ReinSaat - Kürbis > Speisekürbis (Edible Pumpkins/Squash)](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/),
  listed there as `Hokkaido Orange`. **Identity note:** this differs
  botanically from `Butternut` (`Cucurbita moschata`), even though both are
  filed under the same ReinSaat shop category; see the general `Kürbis` note
  for the full discussion of this nuance.
- Pear-shaped fruits, 2-3 kg at maturity, orange-yellow flesh with a
  chestnut-like flavor; the peel softens during cooking and is edible, so the
  fruit does not need peeling before use
  ([ReinSaat - Hokkaido Orange](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/hokkaido_orange/)).
- Bred through ReinSaat's own maintenance breeding program; suitable for
  professional growers, market gardeners, and home gardeners. Seeds are
  sensitive to excess moisture and cold
  ([ReinSaat - Hokkaido Orange](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/hokkaido_orange/)).
- Growth duration: 100 days from planting out to first harvest; harvest
  window 28 days; propagation duration 21 days (see `Planning Value
  Derivations`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate in pots from late April, keeping warm; transplant
  after the last frosts once soil has warmed.
- Sowing: late April through May for pot pre-cultivation
  ([ReinSaat - Hokkaido Orange](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/hokkaido_orange/)).
- Outdoor planting: from mid-May, once soil temperature reaches at least
  10-12 °C and frost risk has passed
  ([ReinSaat - Hokkaido Orange](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/hokkaido_orange/)).

### Direct Sowing
- Description: sow once soil has warmed sufficiently and frost risk has
  passed; seeds are sensitive to excess moisture and cold, so avoid sowing
  into cold, wet soil.
- Sowing: from mid-May
  ([ReinSaat - Hokkaido Orange](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/hokkaido_orange/)).
- Sowing depth: 2-3 cm
  ([ReinSaat - Hokkaido Orange](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/hokkaido_orange/)).

- Spacing: about 2 m between rows, about 1 m within the row
  ([ReinSaat - Hokkaido Orange](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/hokkaido_orange/)).
  A separate garden-guidance source gives roughly 1 m x 1 m for `Hokkaido`
  specifically
  ([Plantura/samen.de garden guidance, cross-checked](https://samen.de/blog/hokkaido-kuerbis-anbau-von-der-aussaat-bis-zur-ernte.html));
  the ReinSaat figure is used as the primary planning value since it comes
  from the variety's own seed source, with the narrower garden figure noted
  as a plausible closer-spacing alternative for small gardens.
- Site: full sun, warm, wind-sheltered; nutrient-rich, well-drained soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest in autumn once the skin has hardened and the stem has corked;
  fruits store very well after curing
  ([ReinSaat - Hokkaido Orange](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/hokkaido_orange/)).
- Used cooked; skin is edible and softens during cooking, so peeling is
  optional.

## Notes
- Thousand seed weight: 224.64 g per the source's stated portion figure
  ([ReinSaat - Hokkaido Orange](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/hokkaido_orange/)).
- Seed requirement: about 100-140 seeds per are for direct sowing, or
  55-70 seeds per are when pre-cultivated
  ([ReinSaat - Hokkaido Orange](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/hokkaido_orange/)).

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Spacing (2 m x 1 m per the variety's own source) is within the general
  crop's commercial range (1.5-2.5 m x 0.5-1.5 m) and somewhat wider than the
  general note's garden-guidance figure (100-150 cm); kept as the more
  specific variety-sourced value rather than the general range.
- Sowing depth (2-3 cm) matches the general crop note exactly; no deviation.
- Fruit weight (2-3 kg) is notably smaller than `Butternut` (up to 3.5 kg,
  see `pumpkin-butternut.md`), consistent with the general crop note's
  observation that fruit size differs enough between these two varieties to
  keep the general yield open and make the calendar values compromises.
- Growth duration deviates slightly from the general crop: 100 days versus
  105 days (general compromise between the two types); `Hokkaido` is the
  earlier-maturing type.
- Propagation duration deviates: 21 days versus 28 days, based on
  [Plantura](https://www.plantura.garden/gemuese/kuerbis/hokkaido-kuerbis) (seedlings planted out about 3 weeks after sowing).
- Harvest window: 28 days, intentionally reused from the general crop; no
  variety-specific source contradicts it.
- Yield: no kg/m² figure specific to `Hokkaido` was found; the general crop's
  wide 5-15 kg/m² commercial range is not narrowed here for lack of a
  variety-specific source; left open.

## Planning Value Derivations

- Propagation duration: 21 days, from [Plantura](https://www.plantura.garden/gemuese/kuerbis/hokkaido-kuerbis) (planting out about
  3 weeks after sowing). Used as given.
- Growth duration: 100 days, measured from planting out to first harvest.
  [Plantura](https://www.plantura.garden/gemuese/kuerbis/hokkaido-kuerbis) states about 95-120 days from sowing to harvest, which minus
  the 21 days of pre-cultivation is 74-99 days after planting; the calendar
  (planting from mid-May, harvest from about late September when the skin is
  hardened and the stem corked, ReinSaat) points to longer. The upper end of
  the sowing-based range, rounded to 100 days, is used. Inferred, not a
  direct source value. Uncertainty: about +/- 15 days with summer warmth; a
  cool year delays ripeness.
- Harvest window: 28 days, reused from the general crop (September to
  October or November per [Plantura](https://www.plantura.garden/gemuese/kuerbis/hokkaido-kuerbis), one or two harvest passes before
  frost).
