## Short Description
- `Vit` is a corn salad / lamb's lettuce variety (`Valerianella locusta`),
  described as particularly fast and compact growing, dark green, with
  short-stemmed, round, clearly veined leaves and a nutty taste.
- Good, reliable yields and good resistance to downy mildew are reported by
  the breeder.
- Growth duration: about 12 weeks (84 days) total culture duration is given
  by the source for autumn/winter cultivation; see
  `Planning Value Derivations`.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow for autumn and winter harvest; suited to open
  field, raised beds, or greenhouse/foil cultivation.
- Sowing: outdoors from early August to mid-September; greenhouse sowing
  from late September through October.
- Sowing depth: 1 cm.

- Spacing: row spacing about 10-15 cm, matching the general crop's open-field
  range; the source text on exact within-row spacing was inconsistent (see
  `Notes`) and is not repeated as a precise figure here.
- Site: sun; suited to open field, raised bed, or greenhouse/foil
  cultivation (general crop site guidance also applies).

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest rosettes from autumn into winter; suited to open field, raised
  beds, or greenhouse/foil cultivation.

## Notes
- The [ReinSaat](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/salate/feldsalat/vit/)
  product text gives a within-row/row-spacing figure as "10 - 30 x 1 cm row
  spacing 10-15 cm", which is not fully parseable into a clean
  spacing-within-row value. This is documented explicitly as an unclear
  source figure rather than silently resolved; only the 10-15 cm row spacing
  is carried forward as a planning value.
- Reported as high-yielding and robust, with good tolerance to downy mildew,
  per the breeder's variety description.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Crop identity matches the general `Feldsalat`/corn salad note: both are
  `Valerianella locusta`.
- Sowing depth (1 cm) matches the general crop's 1-2 cm range, at the
  shallow end; no contradiction.
- Sowing window (early August to mid-September outdoors) fits inside the
  general crop's "mid-August onward" autumn/winter window; the variety
  source gives a slightly earlier start (early August) which is documented
  here rather than silently aligned to the general note.
- Growth duration: the general crop's 80-day inferred value was itself
  derived from this variety's ~12-week (84-day) figure, so the two are
  intentionally close by construction; documented explicitly rather than
  presented as two independent confirmations.
- Thousand kernel weight: 1.83 g is a variety-specific source value from
  ReinSaat; the general crop note leaves this open at the general level, so
  no comparison value exists yet.
- Yield: left open; no numeric variety-specific figure was found beyond the
  qualitative "good, reliable yields" and "high-yielding" claims.
