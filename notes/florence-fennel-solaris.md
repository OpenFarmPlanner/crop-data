## Short Description
- `Solaris` (F1) is an early bulb fennel (`Foeniculum vulgare var. azoricum`;
  general note `florence-fennel.md`). Per
  [Beringmeier](https://www.beringmeier.de/wurzelgemuese-knollengemuese-saatgut/oeko-wurzel-knolle/Knollenfenchel-Solaris-F1-oeko.html)
  and [Hartmann Brockhaus](https://www.hartmann-brockhaus.de/detail/Fenchel-Solaris-F1):
  round to bulbous bulbs, low susceptibility to cracking and brown edges,
  narrow base making it easy to cut; suited to early and autumn crops.
- Growth duration: about 73 days from planting.

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate as for bulb fennel generally.
- Sowing: early March to end of April (spring crop) and mid-July to
  mid-August (autumn crop). The source labels these as planting dates; the
  distinction from sowing dates is uncertain.
- Outdoor planting: about 6 weeks after sowing (general).

- Spacing: 40 x 25-30 cm (general crop; no variety figure).
- Site: as for general bulb fennel; even moisture.

## Harvest & Use
- Spring crop harvest mid-May to end of June; autumn harvest in October.
