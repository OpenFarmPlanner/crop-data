## Short Description
- `Maxi` is a green bush bean, `Phaseolus vulgaris`, of the "Gluckentyp":
  pods form in dense clusters held above the foliage rather than hanging
  below it, which makes picking easier
  ([Sperli](https://www.sperli.de/buschbohnensamen-maxi-80091/)).
- Mid-early, robust, upright-growing, high-yielding variety with long, round,
  completely string-free pods about 17-20 cm long; light brown seed. Tolerant
  to bean mosaic virus.
- Growth duration: about 63 days from sowing to first pods (conflicting seed-catalog
  figures of 42-56 and 70-75 days, see `Planning Value Derivations`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow into warm soil after the last frost; like other
  bush beans, not usually pre-cultivated.
- Sowing: mid-May to end of July, in successions
  ([Sperli](https://www.sperli.de/buschbohnensamen-maxi-80091/),
  [Saatgut Dillmann](https://saatgut-dillmann.de/produkt/buschbohne-maxi-gruen-bio-saatgut/)).
- Sowing depth: about 2-3 cm
  ([Sperli](https://www.sperli.de/buschbohnensamen-maxi-80091/)).

- Spacing: about 40-60 cm between rows, 8-10 cm within the row
  ([Sperli](https://www.sperli.de/buschbohnensamen-maxi-80091/),
  [Saatgut Dillmann](https://saatgut-dillmann.de/produkt/buschbohne-maxi-gruen-bio-saatgut/)).
- Site: sunny to partial shade, well-drained, nutrient-rich soil.
  Germination needs about 15-25 C and takes roughly 14-21 days.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest from July to September/October, picking pods while young, round,
  and string-free; the Gluckentyp habit (pods held above the leaves) makes
  repeated picking faster and easier on the back.
- Suitable for fresh use, freezing, and canning.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Sowing depth deviates slightly from the general crop: `Maxi` sources give
  2-3 cm, close to typical bush-bean guidance; not treated as a meaningful
  deviation, but documented as variety-specific rather than assumed.
- Spacing (40-60 cm rows, 8-10 cm in-row) is consistent with the general
  crop's "about 40 cm between rows" guidance; the 8-10 cm in-row figure is
  more specific than the general note's "every few cm" and is kept as the
  variety-specific value.
- Growth duration deviates from the general crop: `Maxi` uses 63 days,
  whereas the general note (`bush-bean.md`) now carries 56 days (corrected
  from an earlier 49 days on 2026-09-21). The 7-day deviation follows the
  supplier figures for this mid-early variety (see `Planning Value
  Derivations`).
- Harvest window and yield are reused from the general crop note (11 days
  harvest window, about 1.3 kg/m², re-confirmed against garden and trial
  sources on 2026-09-22). No `Maxi`-specific figures for these were found;
  this is an intentional reuse of the general planning values, not a
  variety-specific source claim. The source describes repeated picking from
  July to October across successive sowings, which does not contradict 11
  days for a single sowing.
- Nutrient demand: reused from the general crop (`low`); the general note
  documents a source conflict (`Schwachzehrer` vs `Mittelzehrer`) that is not
  resolved for `Maxi` specifically either.
- Thousand kernel weight: left open for this variety. The general crop uses
  160 g; `Maxi`'s seed is described as light brown and of ordinary bush-bean
  size, so no contradiction is known, but no source directly measures
  `Maxi` seed weight. Documented as an open question rather than silently
  asserting equality.

## Planning Value Derivations

- Growth duration: 63 days, measured from direct sowing to first pods (no
  propagation). Source conflict, documented explicitly:
  [Sperli](https://www.sperli.de/buschbohnensamen-maxi-80091/) states about
  70-75 days, while [ReinSaat](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/bohnen/buschbohnen/gruenhuelsige_buschbohnen/maxi/)
  states about 6-8 weeks (42-56 days). General guidance sits in between:
  [Gärtner Pötschke](https://www.poetschke.de/buschbohnen-anbauen-aussaat-pflege-und-ernte/)
  gives about eight weeks (56 days), and a general guide summary gives 50-60
  days under good conditions; the early bush bean `Saxa` is quoted at 60-66
  days ([raimund Biogartenbedarf](https://www.biogartenbedarf.de/bio-saatgut/bohnen-saatgut/buschbohne-saxa/)),
  and `Maxi` is described as mid-early, so it should not be faster than `Saxa`.
  63 days is the gap midpoint between the ReinSaat upper bound (56) and the
  Sperli lower bound (70); it is an inferred planning value, not a direct
  source value. Uncertainty: about plus/minus 7 days depending on sowing date
  and soil warmth.
- Harvest window: 11 days, reused from the general crop (LWG Veitshöchheim
  gives 8-14 days per sowing; see above);
  inferred, not a variety-specific source value.
