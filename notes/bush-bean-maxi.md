## Short Description
- `Maxi` is a green bush bean, `Phaseolus vulgaris`, of the "Gluckentyp":
  pods form in dense clusters held above the foliage rather than hanging
  below it, which makes picking easier
  ([Sperli](https://www.sperli.de/buschbohnensamen-maxi-80091/)).
- Mid-early, robust, upright-growing, high-yielding variety with long, round,
  completely string-free pods about 17-20 cm long; light brown seed. Tolerant
  to bean mosaic virus.
- Growth duration: reused from the general `Buschbohne` crop (see
  `Comparison With General Crop Data`); no `Maxi`-specific day count to first
  harvest was found, only a germination-time figure (see below).

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow into warm soil after the last frost; like other
  bush beans, not usually pre-cultivated.
- Sowing: mid-May to end of July, in successions
  ([Sperli](https://www.sperli.de/buschbohnensamen-maxi-80091/),
  [Saatgut Dillmann](https://saatgut-dillmann.de/produkt/buschbohne-maxi-gruen-bio-saatgut/)).
- Sowing depth: about 2-3 cm
  ([Sperli](https://www.sperli.de/buschbohnensamen-maxi-80091/)).

- Spacing: about 40-60 cm between rows, 8-10 cm within the row
  ([Sperli](https://www.sperli.de/buschbohnensamen-maxi-80091/),
  [Saatgut Dillmann](https://saatgut-dillmann.de/produkt/buschbohne-maxi-gruen-bio-saatgut/)).
- Site: sunny to partial shade, well-drained, nutrient-rich soil.
  Germination needs about 15-25 C and takes roughly 14-21 days.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest from July to September/October, picking pods while young, round,
  and string-free; the Gluckentyp habit (pods held above the leaves) makes
  repeated picking faster and easier on the back.
- Suitable for fresh use, freezing, and canning.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Sowing depth deviates slightly from the general crop: `Maxi` sources give
  2-3 cm, close to typical bush-bean guidance; not treated as a meaningful
  deviation, but documented as variety-specific rather than assumed.
- Spacing (40-60 cm rows, 8-10 cm in-row) is consistent with the general
  crop's "about 40 cm between rows" guidance; the 8-10 cm in-row figure is
  more specific than the general note's "every few cm" and is kept as the
  variety-specific value.
- Growth duration, harvest window, and yield are reused from the general crop
  note (`bush-bean.md`: 49 days growth duration, 21 days harvest window, about
  1.3 kg/m² yield). No `Maxi`-specific figures for these were found among the
  sources checked (mostly commercial seed listings); this is an intentional
  reuse of the general planning values, not a variety-specific source claim.
- Thousand kernel weight: left open for this variety. The general crop uses
  160 g; `Maxi`'s seed is described as light brown and of ordinary bush-bean
  size, so no contradiction is known, but no source directly measures
  `Maxi` seed weight. Documented as an open question rather than silently
  asserting equality.
