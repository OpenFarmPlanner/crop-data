## Short Description
- Puntarelle is the Catalogna-type leaf chicory `Cichorium intybus var.
  foliosum`, grown for its tender flowering shoots ("puntarelle") and for
  cut leaves.
- Cool-season, fairly frost-tolerant. Moderate nutrient demand, even moisture.
  Bitter flavour softens after cold.
- Growth duration: about 6 weeks to a tender leaf cut, about 70 days from
  planting to the shoot harvest.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow or pre-cultivate; the seed is a light germinator, so
  cover only lightly.
- Sowing: about May to August for autumn to winter harvest.
- Sowing depth: about 0.5-1 cm (light cover).

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivation gives an even stand for the shoot harvest.
- Outdoor planting: about 4 weeks after sowing.

- Spacing: about 30-40 cm between rows, 25-30 cm within the row.
- Site: sun to light shade; deep, moisture-retentive soil. Winter protection
  lets the shoot harvest continue into early spring.

## Harvest & Use
- Cut tender leaves from about 6 weeks after sowing. The inner flowering shoots
  are cut from autumn onward, and with protection through winter into early
  spring.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration: 70 days as a planning value for the shoot harvest from
  planting. [Plantura](https://www.plantura.garden/gemuese/puntarelle/puntarelle-anbauen)
  gives harvest about 70 days after planting, with a tender-leaf cut possible
  about 6 weeks after sowing. 70 days is used directly; documented as the
  shoot-harvest value. The live variety record
  `Catalogna Puntarelle di Galatina/Medusa` stores 80 days.
- Harvest window: 30 days as a planning value, matching the live variety
  record; the shoot harvest per sowing is fairly short, though protected winter
  crops extend it.
- Yield: left open. No reliable per-m² yield source was found for the shoot
  harvest.
- Harvest method: left open until a yield basis exists.
- Thousand kernel weight: left open; chicory seed is small (roughly 1-1.5 g per
  thousand) but a crop-specific source is needed before storing a value.
