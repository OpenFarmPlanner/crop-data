## Short Description
- White cabbage (`Weißkraut`, `Weißkohl`) is the heading form of
  `Brassica oleracea var. capitata f. alba`, grown for firm storage or
  processing heads (sauerkraut).
- Biennial grown as an annual. High nutrient demand, steady water supply,
  benefits from a 3-4 year brassica rotation break.
- Growth duration differs strongly between early types (about 50 days from
  sowing) and late storage/kraut types (about 120 days from sowing).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate and transplant for an even stand. Storage cabbage
  is the main use here.
- Sowing: about March to May for autumn and storage harvest.
- Outdoor planting: about 5-6 weeks after sowing.

- Spacing: about 50-60 cm between rows, 40-50 cm within the row for large
  storage heads.
- Site: full sun, deep nutrient-rich soil, consistent moisture. Net cover
  against cabbage fly, cabbage white, and aphids.

## Harvest & Use
- Cut heads when firm. Storage types are harvested in a short autumn window
  before hard frost and keep for weeks to months in a cool cellar.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration: 120 days as the general planning value for late
  storage/kraut white cabbage from a spring sowing. Garden sources give roughly
  50 days for early types and about 120 days for mid-late and late types
  ([Gartenjournal](https://www.gartenjournal.net/weisskohl-anbauen),
  [Plantura](https://www.plantura.garden/gemuese/weisskohl/weisskohl-pflanzen)).
  The stored crop is the storage type, so the late-type value is used; early
  types deviate strongly. The live variety record `Türkis` also stores 120 days.
- Harvest window: about 45 days is kept as the stored general value; storage
  cabbage is cut over a fairly short autumn window.
- Yield: about 5.0 kg/m² is the stored general value (heavy heads); not
  re-derived here.
- Propagation duration: about 42 days (pre-cultivation), already stored.
