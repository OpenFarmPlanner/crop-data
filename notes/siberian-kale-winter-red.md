## Short Description
- `Winter Red` is a Siberian-kale variety best mapped under the crop
  `Schnittkohl` / `Siberian kale` (`Brassica napus var. pabularia`).
- Botanical identity: `Brassica napus ssp. napus var. pabularia` from
  [ReinSaat - Winter Red](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/kohlgewaechse/gruenkohl_schnittkohl/winter_red/).
- Feathered leaves with magenta stems; harvested at several stages as baby
  leaf, leaf, and stem vegetable.
- Growth duration: 55 days as a planning value for developed-leaf harvest,
  reused from the general crop (see `Planning Value Derivations`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate from February for an early spring stand; also used
  for autumn and overwintering crops.
- Sowing: from about February under protection.
- Outdoor planting: about 5-6 weeks after sowing (category guidance).

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow for cut-leaf and baby-leaf harvest.
- Sowing: from about March outdoors.
- Sowing depth: 1 cm (ReinSaat gives 1 cm for this variety; the category range
  is 1-2 cm).

### Overwintering Crop
- Description: sow later and keep plants small for better winter hardiness in
  rough sites; protect with mulch.
- Sowing: about August to September.
- Harvest: through winter in mild spells into early spring.

- Spacing: 50 cm between rows, 10 cm within the row, following the
  variety-specific figure from ReinSaat.
- Site: sun to partial shade; moist, nutrient-rich, well-drained soil. High
  nutrient demand.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest leaves and stems at baby-leaf, leaf, or mature stage; suited to
  repeated cut-and-come-again picking.
- Germination temperature about 15-20 C.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Crop identity matches the general `Schnittkohl` / `Siberian kale` note: both
  are `Brassica napus var. pabularia`.
- Spacing deviates from the general crop on purpose: the general crop leaves
  spacing open, and `Winter Red` uses the variety-specific 50 x 10 cm from
  [ReinSaat - Winter Red](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/kohlgewaechse/gruenkohl_schnittkohl/winter_red/),
  which gives about 20 plants/m2.
- Sowing depth deviates on purpose: 1 cm is the variety-specific value; the
  general crop keeps the 1-2 cm range.
- Harvest window (120 days) is reused from the general crop. No reliable
  variety-specific harvest-duration value was found; the general winter
  cut-and-come-again planning value was intentionally reused and no
  variety-specific source contradicts it.
- Yield (1.5 kg/m2) is reused from the general crop. No variety-specific yield
  source was found; the general conservative planning value was intentionally
  reused. Any future value should separate baby-leaf from developed-leaf
  harvest.
- Thousand kernel weight deviates with a variety source: about 2.86 g from
  ReinSaat, versus the 3-4 g category figure in the general note.
- Nutrient demand: reused as `high` from the general crop; no contradicting
  variety source.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration: 55 days, intentionally equal to the general `Schnittkohl`
  planning value. ReinSaat gives no single value for `Winter Red`. General
  `Red Russian` type sources give "29 baby; 50 full size" days from direct
  seeding ([Johnny's Selected Seeds](https://www.johnnyseeds.com/vegetables/kale/red-russian-kale-seed-363.html)),
  about 50-60 days to full size
  ([RHS](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/173056/brassica-napus-pabularia-group-red-russian/details),
  [Gardener's Path](https://gardenerspath.com/plants/vegetables/grow-red-russian-kale/)),
  and baby leaf around 25-30 days. `Winter Red` is a standard Red Russian type
  and no variety-specific source deviates from this range, so the general
  55-day value was intentionally reused rather than inventing a variety value.
  The previous live record stored 75 days, an older project value without a
  source; it was replaced with 55 during the 2026-09-04 sync.
- Harvest window: 120 days, reused from the general crop as a winter
  cut-and-come-again planning value; ReinSaat gives a November-to-March range
  for the kale group and an overwintering slot for `Winter Red`.
- Yield: 1.5 kg/m2 (`per_sqm`), reused from the general crop; species-transferred
  from curly-kale figures at [Open Organic Farm](https://openorganic.farm/plants/30)
  because no Siberian-kale-specific source was found. Flagged uncertain.
- Seed requirement, pre-cultivation: 1 seed per plant as a whole-number form
  value. Safety buffer 10 percent kept separate from this base requirement.
- Seed requirement, direct sowing: left open; no reliable `Winter Red`-specific
  sowing rate in g/m2 was found.
- Sowing depth: 1 cm; direct variety value from ReinSaat.
