## Short Description
- `Gigante d'Italia` is a flat-leaf (glatt) parsley variety, `Petroselinum
  crispum var. neapolitanum`. Kultur: Glatte Petersilie (English name
  Flat-leaf parsley); type flat-leaf, confirmed. See `flat-leaf-parsley.md`.
- Large-leaved, strongly aromatic Italian cutting-parsley type with big,
  smooth, dark green leaves and sturdy stems; robust, vigorous, high-yielding
  growth to about 30-50 cm; can be cut repeatedly as long as the growing
  point is not damaged
  ([Saat & Gut](https://www.saat-und-gut.de/bio-petersilie-glatt-gigante-ditalia.html),
  [Quedlinburger Saatgut](https://www.quedlinburger-saatgut.de/saatgut/artikel/petersilie-gigante-ditalia-292919.html)).
- Growth duration: 85 days from direct sowing to first cut; harvest window 70
  days. Both are the general flat-leaf values, intentionally reused.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow into a fine, moist seedbed; biennial, suitable for
  year-round cultivation with staggered sowings.
- Sowing: 2026-03-01 to 2026-07-31 (general flat-leaf window, reused). A July
  sowing is specifically recommended for an autumn crop by
  [Saat & Gut](https://www.saat-und-gut.de/bio-petersilie-glatt-gigante-ditalia.html);
  this is variety-sourced and lies inside the general window.
- Sowing depth: 1.5 cm (1-2 cm), general value.

- Spacing: 25 cm between rows; 3 cm within the row (general commercial spacing,
  reused; the retailer garden spacing of 30 x 20 cm is wider).
- Site: sun to partial shade; humus-rich, evenly moist soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Cut leaves repeatedly, always leaving the heart/growing point intact; large
  leaf size and sturdy stems make picking efficient.
- Fresh use; described as having a more intense flavor than curly parsley and
  being easier to clean.
- Not suited for overwintering: the DLR variety table marks the Gigante types
  as bolting early (see the general note).

- Propagation (45 days), sowing depth, spacing (25 x 3 cm): intentional reuse.

- Yield: 6 kg/m² per season. Described as high-yielding, but no per-m² figure

- Thousand kernel weight: 1.5 g, intentional reuse.
- Nutrient demand: high, intentional reuse (previous "medium" came from the

- Frost: no variety data; general note applies.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration 85 days, harvest window 70 days, yield 6 kg/m²: reused from
  `flat-leaf-parsley.md` (85 = rounded midpoint of 80-92 days; 70 = first cut
  plus two regrowth cuts at 35 days; 3.0 + 1.6 + 1.6 rounded), based on the
  [DLR Rheinpfalz sheet](https://www.bbs-landwirtschaft.de/Internet/global/themen.nsf/0/1ef319fcf05534dbc125876e002d5a6f/$FILE/Petersilie,%20Blatt.pdf)
  and Hortipendium. Not presented as `Gigante d'Italia`-specific source
  claims. Uncertainty about +/- 10 days.
