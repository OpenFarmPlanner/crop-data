## Short Description
- `President` is a celeriac (`Knollensellerie`) variety, `Apium graveolens var.
  rapaceum` (general note: `celeriac.md`; stalk celery is a separate crop,
  see `celery.md`).
- Forms round, smooth tubers with white flesh, upright dark green foliage,
  easy to harvest and well suited to storage, according to
  [Wunderlich Bio-Jungpflanzen](https://www.bio-jungpflanzen.com/Bio-Selleriepflanze-President-im-3-8-cm-Erdballen/41956-3-8).
  The same source recommends planting from mid-May and 25-30 cm spacing.
- Growth duration: no variety-specific day count found; general crop value
  reused (see below).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: as for celeriac generally; light germinator, pre-cultivate
  under glass and plant out from mid-May.
- Sowing: about January to March (general crop guidance).
- Outdoor planting: about 8-10 weeks after sowing (general, inferred).

- Spacing: 40 x 40 cm planning value (general crop); the supplier's 25-30 cm
  is a plant distance in the row only and is noted as a deviation, not adopted
  (see comparison).
- Site: sun, deep humus-rich, evenly moist soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest in autumn (September to October) before hard frost; stores well
  cool and moist into late winter.
