## Short Description
- `Mooskrause` is a curly-leaf (kraus) parsley variety, `Petroselinum crispum`.
  Kultur: Krause Petersilie (English name Curly parsley); type curly,
  confirmed. See `curly-parsley.md`. The name is also used as a generic type
  name for moss-curled parsley cultivars, which is flagged below as an identity
  question.
- Dark green, strongly crinkled ("moss-curled") leaves, described as early to
  mid-maturing, compact, and mild in aroma
  ([Sativa](https://www.sativa.bio/de/krause-petersilie-mooskrause-kraeuter-kr35),
  [naturadb](https://www.naturadb.de/pflanzen/petroselinum-crispum-mooskrause/)).
- Growth duration: 90 days from direct sowing to first cut; harvest window 84
  days for repeated cuts. Both are the values of the general curly-parsley
  note, intentionally reused. See Planning Value Derivations.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow into a fine, moist seedbed; suitable for field beds,
  raised beds, and pots or balcony boxes.
- Sowing: 2026-04-01 to 2026-07-15 (general curly-parsley window, reused).
  Sativa and naturadb texts mention covered sowing from March and again from
  early September and outdoor sowing to early August, but these are general
  parsley statements, not variety data.
- Sowing depth: 1.5 cm (1-2 cm), general curly-parsley value.

- Spacing: 25 cm between rows; 3 cm within the row (general commercial
  spacing, reused; garden guides use wider spacing).
- Site: sun to partial shade; free-draining, humus-rich soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Cut leaves repeatedly once established, always leaving the growing point
  intact; the compact, tightly curled habit suits both bed and container
  harvest.
- Fresh use preferred; mild aroma compared to some other curly types.

## Notes
- "Mooskrause" is both a specific named variety sold by several seed houses
  and a generic descriptive term for moss-curled parsley types in some garden
  literature. This note treats it as the named variety; see "Research Status".

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration and harvest window: reused from `curly-parsley.md`, where
  they derive from the [DLR Rheinpfalz sheet](https://www.bbs-landwirtschaft.de/Internet/global/themen.nsf/0/1ef319fcf05534dbc125876e002d5a6f/$FILE/Petersilie,%20Blatt.pdf)
  (80 days reference plus about 7 days for curly types, rounded to 90; three
  cuts at 6-week steps = 84 days). Not presented as `Mooskrause`-specific
  claims. Uncertainty about +/- 10 days.
- All other values: reused unchanged; see the general note's derivations.
