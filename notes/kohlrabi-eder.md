## Short Description
- `Eder` (`Eder F1`) is a white-fleshed kohlrabi F1 hybrid,
  `Brassica oleracea var. gongylodes`, bred by Rijk Zwaan and explicitly
  marketed for both field and greenhouse cultivation, matched to the general
  crop note `notes/kohlrabi.md`.
- Positioned by the breeder as suited to early/spring cultivation: strong
  vigor, upright growth habit that makes harvest easy, flat-round bulb with
  good internal quality (low tendency to internal hollowing/woodiness).
- Growth duration: no single breeder-stated day count was found; left open
  here and treated as an early-type value within the general crop's range (see
  `Comparison With General Crop Data`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate for the earliest possible planting, matching the
  variety's greenhouse/early-season suitability; the breeder notes
  greenhouse sowing is possible year-round if temperatures stay at least
  15-18 C.
- Sowing: from about February/March under protection for the earliest crop.
- Outdoor planting: about 4-6 weeks after sowing (general crop guidance; no
  variety-specific figure found).

- Spacing: no variety-specific spacing source found; the general crop's
  standard-type range (about 25 x 25 cm) is reused (see `Comparison With
  General Crop Data`).
- Site: sun; nutrient-rich, well-drained, evenly moist soil. Net cover against
  cabbage fly and flea beetles is useful for the young plants.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest once the flat-round bulb reaches usable size; the breeder highlights
  good internal (core) quality, suggesting the bulb tolerates a somewhat wider
  harvest window than lower-quality standard types before turning woody, but no
  concrete number of days is given.
- Eaten raw or cooked.
