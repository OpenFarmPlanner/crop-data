## Short Description
- `Eder` (`Eder F1`) is a white-fleshed kohlrabi F1 hybrid,
  `Brassica oleracea var. gongylodes`, bred by Rijk Zwaan and explicitly
  marketed for both field and greenhouse cultivation, matched to the general
  crop note `notes/kohlrabi.md`.
- Positioned by the breeder as suited to early/spring cultivation: strong
  vigor, upright growth habit that makes harvest easy, flat-round bulb with
  good internal quality (low tendency to internal hollowing/woodiness).
- Growth duration: 60 days from transplanting, derived from the breeder's
  sowing/planting/harvest calendar; harvest window 14 days; propagation 56
  days (see `Planning Value Derivations`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate for the earliest possible planting, matching the
  variety's greenhouse/early-season suitability; the breeder notes
  greenhouse sowing is possible year-round if temperatures stay at least
  15-18 C.
- Sowing: from about February/March under protection for the earliest crop.
- Outdoor planting: about 8 weeks after sowing in the breeder's early-season
  calendar (mid-January to mid-March sowing, mid-March to mid-April planting).

- Spacing: no variety-specific spacing source found; the general crop's
  standard-type range (about 25 x 25 cm) is reused (see `Comparison With
  General Crop Data`).
- Site: sun; nutrient-rich, well-drained, evenly moist soil. Net cover against
  cabbage fly and flea beetles is useful for the young plants.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest once the flat-round bulb reaches usable size; the breeder highlights
  good internal (core) quality, suggesting the bulb tolerates a somewhat wider
  harvest window than lower-quality standard types before turning woody, but no
  concrete number of days is given.
- Eaten raw or cooked.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Definitions: growth duration is counted from transplanting, propagation is
  sowing to transplanting (as in the general note).
- Growth duration: 60 days. The [Rijk Zwaan](https://www.rijkzwaan.de/kohlrabi/EDER-F1-prdBO10031-ctgCrops.kohlrabi)
  variety page and [Beringmeier](https://www.beringmeier.de/kohl-saatgut/oeko-kohl/kohlrabi-eder-rz-f1-oeko.html)
  give sowing mid-January to mid-March, planting mid-March to mid-April and
  harvest mid-May to mid-June. Planting mid-March to first harvest mid-May is
  about 60 days. Inferred from a calendar, not a stated day count. Later
  (warmer) plantings would be similar or slightly faster.
- Propagation duration: 56 days, midpoint reading of the sowing-to-planting
  gap in that calendar (about 8 weeks in winter). Inferred.
- Harvest window: 14 days, reused from the general note (see Comparison). The
  one-month harvest span in the breeder calendar covers staggered plantings
  and is not a single-planting window.
- Yield: no reliable value; left open.
- Field/form constraint: whole days.
