## Short Description
- `Tango` is a late, robust stalk celery (`Apium graveolens var. dulce`;
  general note `celery.md`, celeriac is separate, see `celeriac.md`). Per
  [Biogartenversand / Hof Jeebel](https://biogartenversand.de/biosaatgut/wurzelgemuese/sellerie/stangensellerie-tango):
  medium-green, long smooth stalks, high yielding, very resistant to bolting
  and heart rot, few side shoots, frost-sensitive, moderate nutrient need.
- Growth duration: 80 days from planting (supplier figure; 150-170 days total
  from sowing is the supplier's culture length); harvest window 21 days;
  propagation duration 56 days (general).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate; surface sow (light germinator).
- Sowing: February to mid-May.
- Outdoor planting: April to early July (about 8 weeks after sowing).

- Spacing: 40 cm between rows and within the row.
- Site: sun, moist humus-rich soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest July to late October, about 80 days after planting.
- Fresh use.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Growth duration: 80 days from planting (variety, source-backed); general
  open. `Conga` about 75 days: the five-day difference is small, so "early vs
  late" refers mainly to the sowing/harvest season, not to a large duration
  gap. Noted as a weak distinction.
- Harvest window: season July to October; per-planting window 21 days
  inferred (equals the general value; `Conga` uses 14 days, see
  `celery-conga.md`), not a source figure.
- Early vs late: an additional check of Hortipendium, Sativa Rheinau, Bejo and
  the suppliers found no source that states a larger duration gap; the 5-day
  difference (75 vs 80 days from planting) is kept. The season differs more:
  `Conga` is listed for planting from late March and harvest from mid-June,
  `Tango` for planting from April and harvest from July, so `Tango` mainly
  extends the season into late October.
- Propagation duration: 56 days (8 weeks), which matches the supplier
  statement of about 8 weeks from sowing to planting.
- All durations are counted from transplanting, as in `celery.md`.
- Spacing 40 x 40 cm matches the general garden value.
- Yield: left open, no variety-specific source; general also open.
- Seeds: about 370 seeds per 0.1 g (supplier), variety-specific.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Harvest window: 21 days, inferred: for planting April to early July plus 80
  days, the stated harvest end in late October leaves about 30 days beyond
  the last planting's harvest start (source-start gap about 11 days); 21 days
  is the middle of that range and equals the general value. Not a source
  claim.
- Propagation duration: 56 days from the supplier's about 8 weeks.
- Seed weight: 0.1 g / 370 seeds is about 0.27 g per 1000 seeds; derived from
  supplier count, not a certified TKG.
