## Short Description
- `Gigante d'Italia` is a flat-leaf (glatt) leaf-parsley variety,
  `Petroselinum crispum`. Typ: glatt (flat-leaf), within the "Petersilie,
  Blatt" Kultur (see `leaf-parsley.md` for the Typ-vs-Kultur reasoning).
- Large-leaved, strongly aromatic Italian cutting-parsley type with big,
  smooth, dark green leaves and sturdy stems; robust, vigorous, high-yielding
  growth to about 30-50 cm; can be cut repeatedly as long as the growing
  point is not damaged
  ([Saat & Gut](https://www.saat-und-gut.de/bio-petersilie-glatt-gigante-ditalia.html),
  [Quedlinburger Saatgut](https://www.quedlinburger-saatgut.de/saatgut/artikel/petersilie-gigante-ditalia-292919.html)).
- Growth duration: reused from the general crop's open guidance (see
  `Comparison With General Crop Data`); sources describe it as fast-growing
  ("raschwüchsig") but give no concrete day count.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow into a fine, moist seedbed; biennial, suitable for
  year-round cultivation with staggered sowings.
- Sowing: about March/April to early September for continuous supply; a July
  sowing is specifically recommended for an autumn crop
  ([Saat & Gut](https://www.saat-und-gut.de/bio-petersilie-glatt-gigante-ditalia.html)).
- Sowing depth: about 0.5-1.5 cm (general crop guidance; no
  variety-specific figure found).

- Spacing: about 20 cm between rows, 8-10 cm within the row (general crop
  guidance).
- Site: sun to partial shade; humus-rich, evenly moist soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Cut leaves repeatedly, always leaving the heart/growing point intact; large
  leaf size and sturdy stems make picking efficient.
- Fresh use; described as having a more intense flavor than curly parsley and
  being easier to clean.
