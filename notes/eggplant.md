## Short Description
- Eggplant / aubergine (`Aubergine`) is `Solanum melongena`, a warm-season
  frost-sensitive fruiting crop, in Central Europe grown under cover or in a
  warm sheltered spot.
- High nutrient demand, warmth-dependent, steady water and feeding during
  fruiting.
- Growth duration is long: roughly 60-90 days from transplant to first fruit,
  plus a long pre-cultivation phase.

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate early and warm (about 22-26 C for germination);
  eggplant needs a long head start.
- Sowing: about February to March under warm protection.
- Outdoor / tunnel planting: after the crop is well established and nights are
  warm, about 8-10 weeks after sowing.

- Spacing: about 50-60 cm each way.
- Site: full sun, warm, wind-sheltered, ideally under cover; rich, well-drained
  soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Pick fruit while the skin is still glossy, over a long summer-to-autumn
  window under good conditions.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Thousand kernel weight: 4.3 g as a planning value. Eggplant seed is about
  250-350 seeds per gram, i.e. roughly 2.9-4 g per thousand
  ([Osborne Seed - Seed Count Chart](https://www.osborneseed.com/pages/seed-count-chart));
  the project's own `Luiza` record measures 4.33 g, so 4.3 g is used for the
  general crop.
