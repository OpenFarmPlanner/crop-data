## Short Description
- `Galine` (also sold as `Galine F1`) is an early-ripening `Aubergine` /
  eggplant (`Solanum melongena`) F1 hybrid with rounded, glossy, dark-violet to
  violet-black fruit, bred for both greenhouse and sheltered outdoor growing
  ([SPERLI/Nebelung - Aubergine Galine F1](https://www.nebelung.de/Aubergine-Galine-F1/00080665-000-00),
  [inhortas.de - Aubergine Galine](https://inhortas.de/aubergine-galine/)).
- The variety is parthenocarpic (self-fertile without pollination), which
  secures fruit set even under cover with limited pollinator activity
  ([inhortas.de - Aubergine Galine](https://inhortas.de/aubergine-galine/)).
- Plants can grow tall, up to about 150 cm, and need staking/support similar to
  other greenhouse eggplant types
  ([SPERLI/Nebelung - Aubergine Galine F1](https://www.nebelung.de/Aubergine-Galine-F1/00080665-000-00)).
- Growth duration: early within the eggplant range; first harvest from July,
  i.e. on the shorter end of the general crop's 60-90 day transplant-to-fruit
  window (see `Comparison With General Crop Data`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate warm, like the general crop; `Galine` is
  specifically recommended for greenhouse cultivation but also does well
  outdoors in a sheltered spot after the danger of frost has passed
  ([SPERLI/Nebelung - Aubergine Galine F1](https://www.nebelung.de/Aubergine-Galine-F1/00080665-000-00)).
- Sowing: about February to April under warm protection
  ([SPERLI/Nebelung - Aubergine Galine F1](https://www.nebelung.de/Aubergine-Galine-F1/00080665-000-00)).
- Outdoor / tunnel planting: once frost has passed (from May); no
  variety-specific week count was found, so the general crop's guidance of
  about 8-10 weeks after sowing is reused (see `Comparison With General Crop
  Data`).

- Spacing: no variety-specific spacing source was found; the general crop
  value of about 50-60 cm each way is reused.
- Site: full sun, warm, wind-sheltered, ideally under cover; rich,
  well-drained soil, matching the general crop.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest from July into September under good conditions
  ([SPERLI/Nebelung - Aubergine Galine F1](https://www.nebelung.de/Aubergine-Galine-F1/00080665-000-00)).
- Pick fruit while the skin is still glossy and deep violet-black, before it
  dulls.

## Notes
- Parthenocarpic/self-fertile: useful under cover where pollinator visits are
  limited; do not read this as a claim about seedlessness, sources only
  describe secured fruit set.
- Support/staking recommended once plants approach their typical height
  (up to about 150 cm).

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration (75 days, from transplanting to first fruit),
  propagation duration (70 days, sowing to transplanting) and harvest window
  (75 days): all three are the general eggplant planning values in
  `notes/eggplant.md`, reused intentionally. The only variety-specific
  evidence is the qualitative "early, harvest July to September" wording from
  the seed suppliers listed below; it does not contradict the general values.
- Field/form constraint: whole integer days.
- Uncertainty: real timing depends on sowing date and whether the crop is
  grown under cover; an early variety in a warm tunnel may start earlier and
  finish earlier than the reused values suggest.
