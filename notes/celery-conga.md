## Short Description
- `Conga` is a fast-growing early stalk celery (`Apium graveolens var. dulce`;
  general note `celery.md`, celeriac is separate, see `celeriac.md`). Bejo
  variety per [Beringmeier](https://www.beringmeier.de/wurzelgemuese-knollengemuese-saatgut/oeko-wurzel-knolle/stangensellerie-conga-oeko.html):
  long smooth medium-green stalks, very good bolting resistance, suited to
  spring and summer culture.
- Growth duration: 75 days from planting (supplier figure); harvest window
  14 days; propagation duration 56 days (general).
- Self-blanching is not stated by the supplier; treat as green type.

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate, plant out after frost.
- Sowing: about late March to mid-July per supplier (listed as sowing/planting
  window; the source does not separate the two, so the window is uncertain).
- Outdoor planting: about 8 weeks after sowing (general).

- Spacing: 40 x 40 cm (general crop; no variety figure found).
- Site: sun, moist humus-rich soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest from mid-June to mid-October (supplier).
- Fresh use.

- Harvest window: 14 days, inferred. Planting late March to mid-July plus 75
  days gives first harvest from mid-June (matches the source) to about
  end of September; the stated harvest end in mid-October leaves about 2 weeks
