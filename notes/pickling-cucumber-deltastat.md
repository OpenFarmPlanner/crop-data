## Short Description
- `Deltastat` is treated here as an `Einlegegurke` (pickling cucumber,
  `Cucumis sativus`), per the crop assignment given for this research task.
  **This identity mapping is lower-confidence than the other cucumber
  varieties researched in this project (`Tanja`, `Arola`)**: `Deltastat` was
  not found in the ReinSaat catalog (checked on the
  [Einlegegurken category page](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/gurken/einlegegurken/),
  which lists only `Liefje` and `Vorgebirgstrauben`), nor under that exact
  spelling anywhere else searched.
- **Open identity question:** a similarly named commercial hybrid,
  `Deltastar F1` (Rijk Zwaan), was found, but it does not clearly match: it is
  marketed as a mini cucumber / greenhouse cucumber (`Gewächshausgurke`) with
  dark green, lightly ribbed fruits about 16-18 cm long, suited to
  continued/summer/autumn/early-spring greenhouse cultivation
  ([JustSeed - Deltastar RZ F1](https://justseed.com/products/rjkcucdel001),
  [Anni's Erdbeershop - Gewächshausgurke Deltastar F1](https://www.annis-erdbeer-shop.de/p/gewaechshausgurke-deltastar-f1-veredelt)).
  This is a different spelling (`Deltastar` vs. `Deltastat`) and a different
  harvest form (16-18 cm greenhouse/mini slicing cucumber vs. the small,
  once-over/multi-pick pickling fruit implied by `Einlegegurke`). This note
  does **not** silently assume `Deltastat` = `Deltastar`; the possible
  connection is documented here as an open question rather than decided.
- Calendar values: growth duration 63 days (from sowing) and harvest window
  45 days, both reused from the general crop; no propagation (direct sown).
- Because no confirmed variety-specific source was found, all cultivation
  guidance below is inherited from the general `Einlegegurke` crop note
  (`pickling-cucumber.md`) rather than stated as `Deltastat`-specific; see
  `Comparison With General Crop Data`.
