## Short Description
- `Helenor` (also sold as `Improved Helenor`) is a rutabaga/swede variety,
  matched to the general crop note `notes/rutabaga.md`.
- Identity finding: confirmed as `Brassica napus` (rutabaga/Steckrübe), **not**
  turnip (`Brassica rapa`, Mairübe/Herbstrübe). This was verified specifically
  rather than inferred from a shop category, because ReinSaat-style Brassica
  "Turnips" shop subcategories can cover either species. Confirmation basis:
  [Johnny's Selected Seeds](https://www.johnnyseeds.com/vegetables/rutabagas/helenor-rutabaga-seed-197.html)
  and [Territorial Seed](https://territorialseed.com/products/helenor) both
  list `Helenor` under "Rutabaga"/"Swede" product lines (not turnip), and
  [Dreschflegel](https://dreschflegel-saatgut.de/steckruebe-helenor) lists it
  under its `Steckrübe, Kohlrübe` (rutabaga/swede) category, consistent across
  three independent seed suppliers. Confidence: high.
- Distinguishing traits: round, uniform roots with purple-red "shoulders"
  (the part above soil level) and pale yellow to light-orange flesh described
  as sweet.
- Growth duration: 95 days from sowing (variety-specific sources); harvest
  window 60 days (see `Planning Value Derivations`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow about 90 days before the intended harvest date, for
  a fall/winter harvest; thin to final spacing after germination.
- Sowing: sow around the last frost date, or count back about 90 days from the
  intended harvest.
- Sowing depth: about 1 cm (converted from the source's 3/8 inch).

- Spacing: about 45-60 cm between rows (converted from 18-24 inch), 15 cm
  within the row after thinning (converted from 6 inch); wider than the
  general crop's range, reflecting a source aimed at larger commercial-style
  roots.
- Site: sun; well-drained soil rich in potash and phosphorus, pH about
  6.4-6.8 (general crop's humus/loam guidance is broadly consistent; the pH
  figure is variety-source-specific and not contradicted elsewhere).

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest after at least two good frosts for best flavor and storage quality;
  cut tops and store washed or unwashed at about 0 C and 95% relative
  humidity for up to 6 months.
- Eaten cooked; roots have a red-topped, round appearance useful for bunching
  or market display.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Crop identity matches the general `Steckrübe` / rutabaga note: both are
  `Brassica napus`; see the identity confirmation above, which was documented
  explicitly per the repository's identity-verification rule rather than
  inferred from a shop category.
- Growth duration (90-100 days, taken as 95 days) is documented directly from
  a variety-specific source and matches the general crop's inferred midpoint
  closely; no meaningful deviation.
- Harvest window: 60 days, intentionally reused from the general note; the
  variety sources only say to harvest after at least two good frosts, so no
  variety-specific day count exists and none contradicts the general value.
- Spacing deviates from the general crop's guidance: the general note gives
  40-50 x 30-40 cm from German garden sources, while this variety's US
  breeder source gives a wider 45-60 x 15 cm (converted from inches). Both are
  kept as documented, source-specific figures rather than merged, since they
  reflect different source traditions (home-garden bed spacing vs. row-crop
  spacing) and the in-row figure in particular differs meaningfully
  (15 cm vs. 30-40 cm).
- Storage guidance (post-frost harvest, cold storage at high humidity) is
  variety-source-specific detail not present in the general note; consistent
  with, and an elaboration of, the general note's own frost/storage guidance.
- Yield: no variety-specific figure was found; the general crop now has a
  derived planning value (4.0 kg/m²), which is intentionally reused here
  despite this variety's much tighter spacing (about 45 x 15 cm vs. the
  general 45 x 35 cm), because no `Helenor`-specific root-weight source was
  found to recompute a variety-specific figure; flagged as an uncertain
  reuse rather than a confirmed match, since the tighter spacing likely
  targets smaller, bunching-suited roots rather than the same per-plant
  weight assumed for the general value.
- Nutrient demand: `medium`, intentionally reused from the general note; no
  variety-specific source found.
- Thousand kernel weight: 3.0 g, intentionally reused from the general note;
  no variety-specific figure found.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration: 95 days, taken from
  [Johnny's Selected Seeds](https://www.johnnyseeds.com/vegetables/rutabagas/helenor-rutabaga-seed-197.html)
  ("90 days" from direct seeding) and
  [Pinetree Garden Seeds](https://www.superseeds.com/products/rutabaga-helenor-conventional)
  ("100 Days"). 95 is used as a whole-day midpoint between two directly
  source-stated values; documented as a small inferred adjustment, not a pure
  single-source copy.
- Harvest window: 60 days, reused from the general note (see Comparison).
  Growth duration is counted from direct sowing, as in the general note.
- Field/form constraint: whole days.
- Spacing: sowing depth (3/8 in -> about 1 cm), row spacing (18-24 in -> about
  45-60 cm), and in-row spacing (6 in -> 15 cm) are unit conversions from
  [Johnny's Selected Seeds](https://www.johnnyseeds.com/vegetables/rutabagas/helenor-rutabaga-seed-197.html)
  ("sow 6 seeds per foot, 3/8 in deep, thin to 6 in apart, rows 18-24 in
  apart"), rounded to whole centimeters.
- Yield, nutrient demand and thousand kernel weight: reused from the general
  note (see `Comparison With General Crop Data`).
