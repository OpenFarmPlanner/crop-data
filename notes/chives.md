## Short Description
- Chives (`Schnittlauch`) are `Allium schoenoprasum`, a perennial bunching herb
  grown for its hollow, tubular leaves. Commercial seed lines are usually sold
  by leaf/tube thickness (fine, medium, coarse), which is a variety trait, not
  a separate crop identity.
- Hardy perennial, cut repeatedly over the season; leaves regrow from small
  bulbs after each cut. Moderate nutrient demand, calcareous, humus-rich,
  evenly moist soil preferred
  ([LWG Bayern](https://www.lwg.bayern.de/gartenakademie/gartendokumente/infoschriften/157754/index.php)).
- Growth duration: first cut about 8-10 weeks after spring sowing in the
  establishment year; established plants regrow within about 3-4 weeks between
  cuts and can be cut 3-5 times per season from the second year on
  ([LWG Bayern](https://www.lwg.bayern.de/gartenakademie/gartendokumente/infoschriften/157754/index.php)).

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow in rows; thin seedlings; chives are slow to
  establish in year one but persist as a perennial clump afterward.
- Sowing: about March to August.
- Sowing depth: about 0.5-1 cm.

- Spacing: about 20 cm between rows, 20 cm within the row for clump
  establishment (commercial seedling spacing figures vary; a variety-specific
  figure is used in the `Polyvert` note).
- Site: sun to partial shade, any reasonably fertile, moisture-retentive soil;
  water during dry spells.

## Harvest & Use
- Cut leaves to about 2 cm above ground; harvest possible outdoors from about
  March/April to November. In the sowing year, usually only one cut is taken;
  from the second year, three to five cuts per season are typical
  ([LWG Bayern](https://www.lwg.bayern.de/gartenakademie/gartendokumente/infoschriften/157754/index.php)).
- Used fresh, frozen, or dried; not commonly cooked (loses flavor when heated).

## Notes
- Purple flowers are edible and ornamental but reduce leaf regrowth if left to
  bloom; cutting back before/at flowering keeps leaf quality high.
- Regular fertilization after each cut supports fast regrowth.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration: 63 days used as an inferred planning value for the first
  cut after spring sowing, from the "8-10 weeks to first cut" range implied by
  commercial seedling descriptions (see `Polyvert` note); documented as
  inferred because no single-figure source was found for the general crop.
- Harvest window: left open for the general crop. Cutting continues over
  months in an established stand, so a single harvest-window figure would be
  falsely precise across the establishment year vs. later years; see the
  `Polyvert` note for a variety-specific figure instead.
- Yield: left open. No reliable leaf-yield-per-m² source was found; commercial
  chive yield depends heavily on cutting frequency, stand age, and season.
