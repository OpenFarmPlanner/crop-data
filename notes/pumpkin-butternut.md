## Short Description
- `Butternut` (this note covers the `Waltham`-type widely sold by ReinSaat as
  `Butternut Waltham`) is `Cucurbita moschata`, confirmed via
  [ReinSaat - Kürbis > Speisekürbis (Edible Pumpkins/Squash)](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/).
  **Identity note:** this differs botanically from `Hokkaido` (`Cucurbita
  maxima`), even though both are filed under the same ReinSaat shop category;
  see the general `Kürbis` note for the full discussion.
- Pear-shaped fruits with a thick, seedless neck about 30 cm long, up to
  3.5 kg; firm yellow-orange flesh
  ([ReinSaat - Butternut Waltham](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/butternut_waltham/)).
  Fully ripened fruits store very well.
- Suitable for professional growers, market gardeners, and home gardeners;
  approved for open-field and protected cultivation; Bio Austria certified
  ([ReinSaat - Butternut Waltham](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/butternut_waltham/)).
  The source notes high sensitivity to moisture and cold, and general garden
  guidance notes `Butternut` needs a longer warm period than `Hokkaido`
  ([samen.de - Butternut-Kürbis-Sorten](https://samen.de/blog/butternut-kuerbis-sorten-ein-umfassender-vergleich-fuer-ihren-garten.html)).
- Growth duration: not given as a day-count by the source; left open, matching
  the general `Kürbis` crop (see `Comparison With General Crop Data`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate in pots, keeping warm; transplant only after
  last frosts, since the variety is notably sensitive to cold and moisture.
- Sowing: late April to mid-May for pot pre-cultivation
  ([ReinSaat - Butternut Waltham](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/butternut_waltham/)).
- Outdoor planting: from mid-May, once soil temperature is at least 12 °C and
  frost risk has passed
  ([ReinSaat - Butternut Waltham](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/butternut_waltham/)).

### Direct Sowing
- Description: sow once soil has warmed to at least 12 °C; avoid cold, wet
  soil given the variety's stated sensitivity.
- Sowing: from mid-May
  ([ReinSaat - Butternut Waltham](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/butternut_waltham/)).
- Sowing depth: 2-3 cm
  ([ReinSaat - Butternut Waltham](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/butternut_waltham/)).

- Spacing: 150 x 50-100 cm
  ([ReinSaat - Butternut Waltham](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/butternut_waltham/)).
- Site: full sun, warm, wind-sheltered; nutrient-rich, well-drained soil;
  needs a longer sustained warm period than `Hokkaido`.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest in autumn (source growing calendar indicates September-October)
  once fruits are fully ripe; the thick neck is seedless and the fruit stores
  very well after full ripening
  ([ReinSaat - Butternut Waltham](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/butternut_waltham/)).
- Used cooked; firm yellow-orange flesh, commonly used for soups and roasting.

## Notes
- Seed requirement: about 100-140 seeds per are for direct sowing
  ([ReinSaat - Butternut Waltham](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/butternut_waltham/)).
- The source's growing calendar places pre-cultivation in April-May, field
  planting in May-June, and harvest in September-October, but does not give a
  day-count growth duration.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Spacing (150 x 50-100 cm) sits within the general crop's commercial range
  (1.5-2.5 m x 0.5-1.5 m) and is wider than typical general garden guidance
  (100-150 cm); kept as the more specific variety-sourced value.
- Sowing depth (2-3 cm) matches the general crop note exactly; no deviation.
- Fruit weight (up to 3.5 kg) is notably larger than `Hokkaido` (2-3 kg, see
  `pumpkin-hokkaido.md`), consistent with the general crop note's observation
  that fruit size differs enough between these two varieties to keep yield
  and calendar values open at the general level. This also matches the
  species difference (`C. moschata` vs. `C. maxima`) documented in the
  general note.
- Cold/moisture sensitivity is documented as notably higher for `Butternut`
  than implied for `Hokkaido` by general garden sources; this is a
  variety-level nuance not captured in the general crop note's shared
  cultivation description, and is flagged here rather than silently ignored.
- Growth duration and harvest window: no `Butternut`-specific day-count
  found; left open, consistent with the general `Kürbis` note's open value.
  This is an intentional non-decision, not a reuse of a general planning
  value (the general note has none to reuse).
- Yield: no kg/m² figure specific to `Butternut` was found; the general
  crop's wide 5-15 kg/m² commercial range is not narrowed here for lack of a
  variety-specific source; left open.
