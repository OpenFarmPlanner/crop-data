## Short Description
- `Lorenzo` (F1) is researched here as a yellow, round truss/cluster tomato:
  a vigorous indeterminate variety with firm yellow fruit of about 110-135 g,
  good shelf life and taste, and disease resistance to Fusarium wilt races
  1 and 2, Fusarium crown & root rot, tomato mosaic virus, and Verticillium
  wilt races 1 and 2
  ([Paramount Seeds](https://paramountseeds.com/products/lorenzo-truss-tomato);
  [Beekenkamp Ornamentals](https://www.beekenkampornamentals.nl/en/product/lorenzo-9825/)).
  A German nursery lists the same "Lorenzo" designation on a yellow staked
  tomato sold as "Starlias Yellow (Lorenzo)"
  ([Volmary](https://www.volmary-garten.de/gartenpflanzen/stab-tomate-starlias_r-yellow-lorenzo)).
  Note: a different, unrelated red beefsteak-type variety called
  "Hartschaliger Lorenzo" (>200 g, red, oval) also exists in the
  [Tomaten-Atlas](https://www.tomaten-atlas.de/sorten/h/6628-hartschaliger-lorenzo)
  database; this is documented as a duplicate-name/identity risk below and
  is NOT the variety described in this note, since the task specifies
  `Lorenzo` as yellow-fruited.
- Kultur determination: `Salattomate`, reusing the Kultur already created in
  this batch, with medium-high confidence (see Research Status). Fruit
  weight (~110-135 g) and round shape are consistent with the general
  `Salattomate` size class, and the truss/cluster growth habit matches the
  truss cultivation-type note already documented for
  [Rougella](salad-tomato-rougella.md) in the general `Salattomate` crop
  note - both are yellow-vs-red counterparts of the same round, ~100-135 g,
  truss-harvested slicing-tomato class.
- Growth duration: 70 days from transplanting; harvest window 70 days;
  propagation duration 49 days. No variety-specific figure was found, so the
  general `Salattomate` planning values are intentionally reused (see `Comparison
  With General Crop Data`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: Sow indoors, plant out after the last frost with a support
  structure/stake for this indeterminate, vigorous variety.
- Sowing: 02-01 to 04-15 (reused from the general `Salattomate` window; no
  Lorenzo-specific sowing date was published in the sources reviewed)
- Outdoor planting: from mid-May

- Spacing: no Lorenzo-specific figure found; general `Salattomate` range of
  60-90 cm rows x 45-70 cm in-row reused
- Site: full sun, warm and sheltered; suited to spring and fall greenhouse
  crops per the breeder source, as well as garden cultivation

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest by the truss/cluster once fruit is fully yellow and firm.
- Fresh eating and slicing; described as having good shelf life and a
  pleasant taste.

## Notes
- Bred with a broad Fusarium/Verticillium resistance package, useful in
  soils with a history of these wilts.
- The yellow color is purely a fruit-pigment trait; cultivation needs match
  other round truss-type slicing tomatoes.
