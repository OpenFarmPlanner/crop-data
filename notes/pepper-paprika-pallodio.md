## Short Description
- `Pallodio` F1 (also spelled `Palladio` by some sellers - see source-naming
  note below) is a yellow Spitzpaprika-type `Paprika` (`Capsicum annuum`):
  elongated, pointed "Bullhorn"-shaped fruit rather than the blocky/bell shape
  of `Sven`. This is a fruit-shape type note, not a Kultur difference;
  `Pallodio` remains within the general `Paprika` Kultur alongside blocky
  types such as `Sven`
  ([GemüseProfi - Spitzpaprika gelb](https://gemueseprofi.de/jungpflanzen/spitzpaprika-gelb)).
- Sweet, aromatic, crisp fruit, about 5-6 cm wide and 18-22 cm long,
  160-180 g; low, compact plant habit, about 40-100 cm tall; early-maturing
  and productive, suitable for open field, balcony, or greenhouse
  ([GemüseProfi - Spitzpaprika gelb](https://gemueseprofi.de/jungpflanzen/spitzpaprika-gelb)).
- High heat requirement and heavy feeder (Starkzehrer), consistent with the
  general `Paprika` crop's nutrient demand
  ([GemüseProfi - Spitzpaprika gelb](https://gemueseprofi.de/jungpflanzen/spitzpaprika-gelb)).
- Growth duration: 74 days from transplanting. The source gives "9-12
  weeks" development time from planting out, not from sowing; harvest window
  90 days (August to October) and propagation duration 70 days (reused from
  the general crop). See `Planning Value Derivations`; the growth duration is
  an inferred planning value, not a direct source day count.

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate early and warm, like the general `Paprika`
  crop; the source describes planting out timing rather than sowing timing in
  detail.
- Sowing: late February to early March (reused from the general `Paprika`
  note; no `Pallodio`-specific sowing date was found).
- Outdoor planting: May to June, after the last frost
  ([GemüseProfi - Spitzpaprika gelb](https://gemueseprofi.de/jungpflanzen/spitzpaprika-gelb)).
- Sowing depth: about 1 cm (reused from the general `Paprika` note; no
  `Pallodio`-specific depth figure was found).

- Spacing: about 40 x 40 cm between plants (reused from the general `Paprika`
  note; no `Pallodio`-specific spacing figure was found).
- Site: sunny, warm, sheltered location or greenhouse; nutrient-rich soil,
  matching the general `Paprika` crop's requirements and the source's
  "Starkzehrer" note
  ([GemüseProfi - Spitzpaprika gelb](https://gemueseprofi.de/jungpflanzen/spitzpaprika-gelb)).

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest August, September, and October
  ([GemüseProfi - Spitzpaprika gelb](https://gemueseprofi.de/jungpflanzen/spitzpaprika-gelb)).
- Fruit is sweet and crisp; used fresh (raw) or cooked.

## Notes
- Crop rotation: avoid growing after other Solanaceae on the same spot for
  several years, as documented in the general `Paprika` note.
- Naming: some sellers spell this variety `Palladio` (double `l`) and others
  `Pallodio`; both refer to the same yellow Spitzpaprika F1 variety in the
  sources checked. This spelling variation is documented here rather than
  silently normalized, since no authoritative breeder registration record was
  checked to confirm the canonical spelling.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Growth duration: `Pallodio`'s source gives "9-12 weeks" (63-84 days)
  after planting out. The general `Paprika` note uses 70 days; this variety
  uses 74 days, the midpoint of its own source range, as a small
  variety-specific deviation (see `Planning Value Derivations`).
- Harvest window: `Pallodio` sources give August-October, matching the
  general `Paprika` note's August-to-October guidance; the general value of 90
  days is intentionally reused.
- Propagation duration: no `Pallodio`-specific figure; the general 70 days is
  intentionally reused.
- Spacing and early sowing timing: reused from the general `Paprika` note (40
  x 40 cm, late February to early March sowing). No `Pallodio`-specific
  source contradicts these, and no `Pallodio`-specific source gives its own
  figures either.
- Yield: the general `Paprika` note now sets 5.5 kg/m² (open field to
  unheated tunnel; see `pepper-paprika.md`). No kg/m² or kg/plant figure was
  found for `Pallodio` by name; a 2023 unheated-tunnel trial at LWG Bamberg
  of 5 other pointed (Spitzpaprika) varieties gives 0.77-1.7 kg/plant
  marketable, with the yellow-ripening `Yosemite F1` reaching 1.6 kg/plant -
  the same yellow Spitzpaprika fruit-shape type as `Pallodio`, but a
  different named cultivar, so this is documented as supporting context only,
  not a `Pallodio`-specific source claim
  ([LWG Bamberg - Spitzpaprika-Sortenvergleich 2023](https://www.lwg.bayern.de/mam/cms06/gartenbau/dateien/spitzpaprika_sortenvergleich_im_ungeheizten_tunnel_2023.pdf)).
  The general 5.5 kg/m² value is intentionally reused for `Pallodio`; no
  variety-specific source contradicts it.
- Nutrient demand: the general `Paprika` note sets "high" (Starkzehrer),
  consistent with this variety's own "Starkzehrer" note in its Short
  Description
  ([GemüseProfi](https://gemueseprofi.de/jungpflanzen/spitzpaprika-gelb),
  [Plantura - Starkzehrer](https://www.plantura.garden/gemuese/gemuese-anbauen/starkzehrer-mittelzehrer-und-schwachzehrer)).
  Directly source-backed for this variety, not just a reuse.
- Fruit weight and length (160-180 g, 18-22 cm) and fruit shape (Spitzpaprika)
  are `Pallodio`-specific and have no general-crop equivalent to compare
  against.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration: 74 days from transplanting. Source basis: "9-12 weeks"
  (63-84 days) after planting out in May-June
  ([GemüseProfi](https://gemueseprofi.de/jungpflanzen/spitzpaprika-gelb)).
  Derivation: midpoint 73.5 days, rounded up to 74. The reading matches the
  stated harvest period (planting in May/June plus 9-12 weeks lands in
  July-September). Inferred, not a direct source value.
- Harvest window: 90 days from the August, September and October harvest
  months named by the source (about 92 days, rounded down to 90; equals the
  general value).
- Propagation duration: 70 days, reused from the general `Paprika` note; no
  variety source.
- Yield: 5.5 kg/m², reused from the general `Paprika` note; the LWG Bamberg
  Spitzpaprika trial data (0.77-1.7 kg/plant for other named pointed
  varieties) is documented as supporting context above but not converted
  into a `Pallodio`-specific figure, since plant density/row spacing for
  that trial is not fully stated and `Pallodio` itself was not among the
  tested cultivars.
- Sowing depth: 1 cm, reused from the general `Paprika` note; no
  `Pallodio`-specific figure was found.
- Field/form constraint: whole integer days.
- Uncertainty: the 63-84 day range is wide; an early planting in a tunnel
  can be at the low end, a cool open-field year at the high end.
