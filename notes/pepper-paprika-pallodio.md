## Short Description
- `Pallodio` F1 (also spelled `Palladio` by some sellers - see source-naming
  note below) is a yellow Spitzpaprika-type `Paprika` (`Capsicum annuum`):
  elongated, pointed "Bullhorn"-shaped fruit rather than the blocky/bell shape
  of `Sven`. This is a fruit-shape type note, not a Kultur difference;
  `Pallodio` remains within the general `Paprika` Kultur alongside blocky
  types such as `Sven`
  ([GemüseProfi - Spitzpaprika gelb](https://gemueseprofi.de/jungpflanzen/spitzpaprika-gelb)).
- Sweet, aromatic, crisp fruit, about 5-6 cm wide and 18-22 cm long,
  160-180 g; low, compact plant habit, about 40-100 cm tall; early-maturing
  and productive, suitable for open field, balcony, or greenhouse
  ([GemüseProfi - Spitzpaprika gelb](https://gemueseprofi.de/jungpflanzen/spitzpaprika-gelb)).
- High heat requirement and heavy feeder (Starkzehrer), consistent with the
  general `Paprika` crop's nutrient demand
  ([GemüseProfi - Spitzpaprika gelb](https://gemueseprofi.de/jungpflanzen/spitzpaprika-gelb)).
- Growth duration: the source gives "9-12 weeks" development time from
  planting out, not from sowing; see `Planning Value Derivations` for how
  this was interpreted, and note this is an inferred planning value, not a
  direct source day-count from sowing.

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate early and warm, like the general `Paprika`
  crop; the source describes planting out timing rather than sowing timing in
  detail.
- Sowing: late February to early March (reused from the general `Paprika`
  note; no `Pallodio`-specific sowing date was found).
- Outdoor planting: May to June, after the last frost
  ([GemüseProfi - Spitzpaprika gelb](https://gemueseprofi.de/jungpflanzen/spitzpaprika-gelb)).

- Spacing: about 40 x 40 cm between plants (reused from the general `Paprika`
  note; no `Pallodio`-specific spacing figure was found).
- Site: sunny, warm, sheltered location or greenhouse; nutrient-rich soil,
  matching the general `Paprika` crop's requirements and the source's
  "Starkzehrer" note
  ([GemüseProfi - Spitzpaprika gelb](https://gemueseprofi.de/jungpflanzen/spitzpaprika-gelb)).

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest August, September, and October
  ([GemüseProfi - Spitzpaprika gelb](https://gemueseprofi.de/jungpflanzen/spitzpaprika-gelb)).
- Fruit is sweet and crisp; used fresh (raw) or cooked.

## Notes
- Crop rotation: avoid growing after other Solanaceae on the same spot for
  several years, as documented in the general `Paprika` note.
- Naming: some sellers spell this variety `Palladio` (double `l`) and others
  `Pallodio`; both refer to the same yellow Spitzpaprika F1 variety in the
  sources checked. This spelling variation is documented here rather than
  silently normalized, since no authoritative breeder registration record was
  checked to confirm the canonical spelling.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration: the source states "9-12 weeks" (63-84 days) development
  time after planting out in May-June, harvesting August-October. This
  matches the stated harvest window (planting in May/June + 9-12 weeks lands
  in July-September, extending into October as later-set fruit ripens). A
  planning value of about 70-80 days from transplanting is a plausible
  midpoint reading of the source range, but is presented here only as a
  derivation, not as a direct source value; the exact figure to store (if
  any) should be chosen at sync time with this uncertainty documented.
- Field/form constraint: if a single integer growth-duration value is
  required for live sync, a value in the 63-84 day range should be chosen and
  the rounding documented at that time; no single value is asserted here as
  final.
