## Short Description
- Sugar / snow pea (`Zuckererbse`) is the edible-pod form of `Pisum sativum`,
  a cool-season legume; fixes nitrogen, so nutrient demand is low.
- Prefers cool moist conditions; suffers in heat and drought.
- Growth duration: about 12-14 weeks from sowing to the first pods.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow early; peas dislike transplanting.
- Sowing: about March to early June; an early sowing gives the best crop before
  summer heat and mildew.
- Sowing depth: 3-5 cm.

- Spacing: rows about 40 cm apart (double rows for support), a few cm within
  the row.
- Site: sun to light shade; cool, moisture-retentive, not freshly manured
  soil. Provide support.

## Harvest & Use
- Pick pods young while flat and tender (snow pea) or just as peas swell (snap
  pea), repeatedly over a few weeks.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Thousand kernel weight: 165 g as a planning value. Pea seed spans a wide
  range by type (small wrinkled sugar types roughly 150-250 g per thousand,
  large types more); the project's `Norli` record measures 165.76 g, so 165 g
  is used for the general crop.
- Propagation duration: left empty. Peas are direct sown.
- Yield: 1.0 kg/m² (`per_sqm`). Corrected on 2026-09-22 from a previously
  unverified live value of 1.5 kg/m². Source basis: general search results
  (aggregating sugar/snow-pea garden guidance without one stable named
  institutional source, so treated as moderate-confidence) give a range of
  about 500 g to 1 kg per m² for `Zuckererbse`; a general pea reference table
  on [forum.garten-pur.de](https://forum.garten-pur.de/index.php?topic=28046.0)
  gives "Erbsen, grüne Pflück- 0,5-1,2" kg/m² for shelling peas, which sugar
  peas (whole edible pod, heavier per fruit) plausibly reach or exceed. The
  upper end of the directly found sugar-pea range, 1.0 kg/m², is used; 1.5
  kg/m² is above every source checked and is not carried forward. Uncertainty:
  moderate; a dedicated institutional trial figure (comparable to the Saxon
  `Markerbse` trial used for `shelling-pea.md`) was not found for
  `Zuckererbse` specifically.
- Within-row distance: 4 cm, matching the `Sweet Horizon` variety's sourced
  4-5 cm ([Bobby-Seeds](https://www.bobby-seeds.com/gemuesesamen/bohnen-erbsensamen/erbsensamen/2409/erbse-sweet-horizon-zuckererbse))
  and general pea in-row guidance; used as the general planning value since no
  contradicting general-crop-specific figure was found.
