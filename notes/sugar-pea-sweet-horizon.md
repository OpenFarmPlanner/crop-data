## Short Description
- `Sweet Horizon` is a snow/sugar pea (`Zuckererbse`), `Pisum sativum`, with
  flat, stringless, dark-green pods noted for sweetness and an extended
  tender-pod window
  ([Bobby-Seeds](https://www.bobby-seeds.com/gemuesesamen/bohnen-erbsensamen/erbsensamen/2409/erbse-sweet-horizon-zuckererbse),
  [Kiepenkerl](https://www.kiepenkerl.de/zuckererbsensamen-sweet-horizon-1005050/)).
- Medium-late, vigorous, sturdy growth; resistant to powdery mildew and leaf
  spot (Ascochyta), with Fusarium wilt resistance also reported by German
  retailers
  ([Bobby-Seeds](https://www.bobby-seeds.com/gemuesesamen/bohnen-erbsensamen/erbsensamen/2409/erbse-sweet-horizon-zuckererbse),
  [Kiepenkerl](https://www.kiepenkerl.de/zuckererbsensamen-sweet-horizon-1005050/),
  [Territorial Seed](https://territorialseed.com/products/pea-sweet-horizon)).
- Growth duration: 90 days from sowing to first pods, intentionally reused
  from the general `Zuckererbse` crop; sources for this variety conflict (see
  `Comparison With General Crop Data`). Harvest window 30 days, also reused.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow; peas dislike transplanting. `Sweet Horizon` grows
  tall enough that climbing support (canes/netting or trellis) is recommended
  by all consulted sources.
- Sowing: about early April to mid-June
  ([Bobby-Seeds](https://www.bobby-seeds.com/gemuesesamen/bohnen-erbsensamen/erbsensamen/2409/erbse-sweet-horizon-zuckererbse),
  [Kiepenkerl](https://www.kiepenkerl.de/zuckererbsensamen-sweet-horizon-1005050/)).
- Sowing depth: about 3-4 cm
  ([Kiepenkerl](https://www.kiepenkerl.de/zuckererbsensamen-sweet-horizon-1005050/));
  Territorial Seed gives about 2.5-4 cm (1-1.5 in)
  ([Territorial Seed](https://territorialseed.com/products/pea-sweet-horizon)).
  Both sit within the general crop's 3-5 cm range.

- Spacing: sources diverge on row spacing. Bobby-Seeds gives 30-40 cm between
  rows with 4-5 cm within the row; Kiepenkerl gives 50 cm between rows with
  3-5 cm within the row
  ([Bobby-Seeds](https://www.bobby-seeds.com/gemuesesamen/bohnen-erbsensamen/erbsensamen/2409/erbse-sweet-horizon-zuckererbse),
  [Kiepenkerl](https://www.kiepenkerl.de/zuckererbsensamen-sweet-horizon-1005050/)).
  Both figures fit inside the general crop's "about 40 cm rows, a few cm
  within the row" guidance; not treated as a real deviation, documented as a
  source spread instead.
- Site: sun to light shade; cool, moisture-retentive soil, not freshly
  manured; provide climbing support. Plant height is reported very
  inconsistently across sources: 60-80 cm
  ([Bobby-Seeds](https://www.bobby-seeds.com/gemuesesamen/bohnen-erbsensamen/erbsensamen/2409/erbse-sweet-horizon-zuckererbse),
  [Kiepenkerl](https://www.kiepenkerl.de/zuckererbsensamen-sweet-horizon-1005050/))
  versus 127-152 cm (50-60 in)
  ([Territorial Seed](https://territorialseed.com/products/pea-sweet-horizon)).
  This is left as an open source conflict (see `Research Status`); no height
  field is currently mapped in `docs/culture-field-mapping.md`, so it does not
  affect a structured value, but it is noted here because it may reflect
  different growing/support conditions or a source error.

## Harvest & Use
- Pick pods young while still flat and before the seeds swell noticeably;
  pods stay tender for an extended window and repeated picking is
  recommended. German retailers report a June/July (Bobby-Seeds) to
  June-September (Kiepenkerl, including succession sowings) harvest season
  ([Bobby-Seeds](https://www.bobby-seeds.com/gemuesesamen/bohnen-erbsensamen/erbsensamen/2409/erbse-sweet-horizon-zuckererbse),
  [Kiepenkerl](https://www.kiepenkerl.de/zuckererbsensamen-sweet-horizon-1005050/)).
  Pods are about 5-10 cm long and weigh about 5-10 g each
  ([Kiepenkerl](https://www.kiepenkerl.de/zuckererbsensamen-sweet-horizon-1005050/)).

## Notes
- Nitrogen-fixing legume, same low nutrient demand as the general crop.
- Reported disease resistance (powdery mildew, leaf spot/Ascochyta, and by
  some sources Fusarium wilt) is a practical advantage over less resistant
  sugar pea varieties, but is taken from retailer marketing copy rather than
  an independent trial source; treated as plausible but not independently
  verified.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Sowing window (early April-mid June) sits within the general crop's "about
  March to early June" guidance; slightly later start, not treated as a
  meaningful deviation.
- Sowing depth (3-4 cm, or 2.5-4 cm per Territorial Seed) is within the
  general crop's 3-5 cm range; reused as consistent, not overridden.
- Row/in-row spacing (30-50 cm rows, 3-5 cm within row, depending on source)
  is within the general crop's "about 40 cm rows, a few cm within the row"
  guidance; treated as consistent, not a deviation.
- Thousand kernel weight deviates clearly upward from the general crop's
  planning value: Bobby-Seeds gives about 240 g for `Sweet Horizon`, versus
  the general crop's 165 g (itself derived from the live `Norli` record, a
  different variety). This is documented as a genuine variety-specific
  deviation, not reused from the general value; see `Planning Value
  Derivations`.
- Growth duration: intentionally reused from the general crop (90 days).
  Variety sources conflict: US retailer Territorial Seed states 60-70 days to
  maturity, whereas German retailers' April sowing with first harvest in
  June/July implies roughly 75-100 days. The general value of 90 days lies
  inside the plausible spread and no variety-specific source contradicts it
  reliably, so it is reused rather than picking one source silently.
- Harvest window: no `Sweet Horizon`-specific day-count source was found.
  Multiple sources describe an extended, repeated-picking harvest, consistent
  with (not contradicting) the general crop's 30-day harvest window planning
  value; that general value is intentionally reused here because no reliable
  variety-specific figure was found and nothing contradicts it.
- Yield: no `Sweet Horizon`-specific kg/m² figure was found. The general
  crop's planning value, corrected on 2026-09-22 from 1.5 kg/m² to 1.0 kg/m²
  (see `sugar-pea.md`), is intentionally reused; no variety-specific source
  contradicts it.
- Within-row distance: the general crop's newly documented 4 cm value matches
  this variety's own sourced range (4-5 cm, Bobby-Seeds) almost exactly; kept
  as the variety-sourced figure.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Thousand kernel weight: 240 g, taken directly from
  [Bobby-Seeds](https://www.bobby-seeds.com/gemuesesamen/bohnen-erbsensamen/erbsensamen/2409/erbse-sweet-horizon-zuckererbse)
  ("ca. 240 Gramm"). No rounding applied; used as given.
- Growth duration: 90 days, reused from the general crop and measured from
  direct sowing to first pods. Source basis: Territorial Seed's 60-70 days
  (US catalog day count, probably for warmer-season sowings) versus the
  implied 75-100 days from German sowing and harvest windows (April sowing,
  harvest from June/July). Derivation: no source is silently preferred; the
  general crop value falls within the credible spread and is used as an
  intentional general planning value. Uncertainty: about +/- 15 days
  depending on sowing date; an early cold-soil sowing is at the upper end.
- Harvest window and yield: both reused directly from the general crop note
  (30 days; 1.0 kg/m², corrected from 1.5 kg/m² on 2026-09-22) as intentional
  general planning values; no variety-specific source was found for either,
  and no variety-specific source contradicts the general values.
