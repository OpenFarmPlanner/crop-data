## Short Description
- `Bolstar` (marketed under multiple fruit-type suffixes such as Sensatica,
  Granda, Gimli - all part of the Bolstar breeding line) is a Roma-type
  sauce tomato: oval-round, plum-shaped fruit on long trusses, harvested as
  single loose fruit rather than on the cluster
  ([Kiepenkerl](https://www.kiepenkerl.de/salat-tomatensamen-bolstar-sensatica-f1-2789/),
  [saemereien.ch](https://www.saemereien.ch/tomatensamen-kaufen-roma-tomate-bolstar-sensatica-lycopersicon-esculentum-tomatensamen)).
  This is a type note, not a separate Kultur: Roma-type and San-Marzano-type
  sauce tomatoes are both filed under `Soßentomate` by
  [ReinSaat](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/tomaten_paradeiser/); see
  [Monterrey](sauce-tomato-monterrey.md) for the San-Marzano-type sibling
  variety in this project.
- Fruit weight: about 70-80 g.
- F1 hybrid (Sensatica/Gimli/Granda are all named F1 lines); vigorous,
  high-yielding, with clusters of up to 12 fruits and good disease
  resistance; well suited to organic protected cultivation (tunnels, lightly
  heated greenhouses).
- Growth duration: 70 days from transplanting; harvest window 60 days;
  propagation duration 49 days. No variety-specific figure was found, so the
  general `Soßentomate` planning values are intentionally reused (see `Comparison
  With General Crop Data`).
- Yield: 10.2 kg/m², directly source-backed for this variety - notably higher
  than the general `Soßentomate` value (see `Comparison With General Crop
  Data` and `Planning Value Derivations`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: Sow indoors, plant out from mid-May at the earliest,
  preferably into a tunnel or lightly heated greenhouse.
- Sowing: 02-15 to 03-31 (reused from the general `Soßentomate` window; no
  Bolstar-specific sowing date was published in the sources reviewed)
- Sowing depth: about 0.5 cm, reused from the general `Soßentomate` note; no
  `Bolstar`-specific figure was found.
- Outdoor planting: from mid-May

- Spacing: 50-60 cm within the row
  ([saemereien.ch](https://www.saemereien.ch/tomatensamen-kaufen-roma-tomate-bolstar-sensatica-lycopersicon-esculentum-tomatensamen));
  row spacing not given in the sources reviewed, general `Soßentomate` range
  of 70-100 cm reused
- Site: tunnel or lightly heated greenhouse preferred for best results; full
  sun, warm, sheltered if grown in the open

## Harvest & Use
- Picked loose (without the calyx/crown) rather than as a truss.
- Used for sauce and paste; excellent flesh quality and good storage life
  are highlighted by the source.

## Notes
- Strong disease resistance and vigor noted for protected/organic
  cultivation; well suited to film tunnels.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Fruit shape and use match the general `Soßentomate` description directly
  (Roma-type, oval-round, sauce use).
- In-row spacing (50-60 cm) is source-backed for this variety and sits at
  the wider end of the general 35-60 cm in-row range; no deviation beyond
  normal variety spread.
- Row spacing: no Bolstar-specific figure was found, so the general
  `Soßentomate` range (70-100 cm) is intentionally reused; the general note
  itself documents this range as an open source conflict, so this reuse
  carries the same uncertainty forward rather than resolving it.
- Sowing window: no Bolstar-specific date found; the general crop's
  February-to-March sowing window is intentionally reused because no
  contradicting variety-specific source was found.
- Growth duration, harvest window and propagation duration: no
  `Bolstar`-specific day counts were found. The general `Soßentomate` note gives
  70 days (transplanting to first harvest), 60 days (harvest window) and 49
  days (sowing to transplanting); these are intentionally reused, and no
  variety-specific source contradicts them. Qualitative wording about
  earliness or vigor in the sources was not converted into a number. This is a
  reuse, not a `Bolstar`-specific source claim.
- Yield: 10.2 kg/m², directly source-backed for `Bolstar` from a 2017
  comparative variety trial by LWK Nordrhein-Westfalen's organic horticulture
  division ("Sortenprüfung von Tomaten im Frühsommer"), which reports this
  exact figure for `Bolstar Sensatica F1`
  ([hortigate - Sortenprüfung von Tomaten im Frühsommer](https://www.hortigate.de/publikation/75714/Sortenpruefung-von-Tomaten-im-Fruehsommer/)).
  This is notably higher than the general `Soßentomate` note's inferred
  6.0 kg/m² planning value (derived from home-garden per-plant estimates).
  Documented as a real, source-backed variety-specific deviation rather than
  a reuse: the trial is an early-summer organic comparison, and the
  cultivation structure (tunnel/greenhouse vs. open field) is not stated
  explicitly in the accessible summary, so the higher figure may partly
  reflect protected/organic-trial conditions rather than typical open-field
  home-garden cultivation; this uncertainty is noted rather than
  silently smoothed into the general value.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration 70 days, harvest window 60 days, propagation duration
  49 days: reused from the general `Soßentomate` note, where the source basis and
  derivation are documented. No `Bolstar` day count was found.
- Field/form constraint: whole integer days.
- Uncertainty: fits a staked crop planted out in mid-May; protected
  cultivation or an earlier variety can start about four weeks earlier.
- Nutrient demand: high, reused from the general `Soßentomate` note; no
  `Bolstar`-specific source contradicts it.
- Sowing depth: 0.5 cm, reused from the general `Soßentomate` note.
