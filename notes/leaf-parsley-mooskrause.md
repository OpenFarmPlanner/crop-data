## Short Description
- `Mooskrause` is a curly-leaf (kraus) leaf-parsley variety, `Petroselinum
  crispum`. Typ: kraus (curly), within the "Petersilie, Blatt" Kultur (see
  `leaf-parsley.md` for the Typ-vs-Kultur reasoning); it is also used as a
  generic type name for moss-curled parsley cultivars, which is flagged below
  as an identity question.
- Dark green, strongly crinkled ("moss-curled") leaves, described as early to
  mid-maturing, compact, and mild in aroma
  ([Sativa](https://www.sativa.bio/de/krause-petersilie-mooskrause-kraeuter-kr35),
  [naturadb](https://www.naturadb.de/pflanzen/petroselinum-crispum-mooskrause/)).
- Growth duration: reused from the general crop's open guidance (see
  `Comparison With General Crop Data`); sources describe it as early to
  mid-maturing but give no concrete day count.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow into a fine, moist seedbed; suitable for field beds,
  raised beds, and pots/balcony boxes.
- Sowing: covered cultivation from about March/April and again from early
  September; outdoor sowing from about April to early August
  ([general parsley sources, see `leaf-parsley.md`]).
- Sowing depth: about 1-2 cm.

- Spacing: about 20 cm between rows, 10 cm within the row.
- Site: sun to partial shade; free-draining, humus-rich soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Cut leaves repeatedly once established, always leaving the growing point
  intact; compact, tightly curled habit suits both bed and container harvest.
- Fresh use preferred; mild aroma compared to some other curly types.

## Notes
- "Mooskrause" is both a specific named variety sold by several seed houses
  and a generic descriptive term for moss-curled parsley types in some garden
  literature. This note treats it as the named variety per the task's variety
  list; see "Research Status" for the resulting identity caveat.
