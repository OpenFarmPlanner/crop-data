## Short Description
- `Sven` (F1) is a yellow, blocky/bell-shaped `Paprika` (`Capsicum annuum`),
  the classic "Blockpaprika" fruit type: compact, boxy fruit rather than the
  elongated, pointed Spitzpaprika shape. This is a fruit-shape type note, not
  a Kultur difference; `Sven` remains within the general `Paprika` Kultur
  alongside pointed types such as `Pallodio`
  ([Rijk Zwaan - SVEN F1](https://www.rijkzwaan.de/paprika-s%C3%BC%C3%9F/SVEN-F1-35-220-prdCA10805-ctgCrops.sweet-pepper)).
- Medium fruit weight of about 180-200 g; compact, vigorous plant with an
  open growth habit and good fruit set, bred for high, uniform yields and
  stable fruit sizes
  ([Rijk Zwaan - SVEN F1](https://www.rijkzwaan.de/paprika-s%C3%BC%C3%9F/SVEN-F1-35-220-prdCA10805-ctgCrops.sweet-pepper)).
- In a variety comparison trial at Weihenstephan-Triesdorf University of
  Applied Sciences, `Sven` stood out positively for good quality and a high
  yield level among yellow blocky paprika varieties.
- No `Sven`-specific day-count growth duration or harvest-window figure was
  found; the breeder page describes yield and fruit quality but not a
  concrete cultivation calendar. Growth duration (70 days), harvest window (90
  days) and propagation duration (70 days) intentionally reuse the general
  `Paprika` planning values (see `Comparison With General Crop Data`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate early and warm, like the general `Paprika`
  crop; no `Sven`-specific sowing guidance beyond the breeder's commercial
  variety page was found.
- Sowing: late February to early March (reused from the general `Paprika`
  note; no `Sven`-specific date found).
- Outdoor / tunnel planting: after the "Ice Saints" (mid-May), once frost
  risk has passed (reused from the general `Paprika` note).

- Spacing: about 40 x 40 cm between plants (reused from the general `Paprika`
  note; no `Sven`-specific spacing figure was found).
- Site: full sun, warm, sheltered; nutrient-rich, well-drained soil, matching
  the general `Paprika` crop's requirements.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest from summer into autumn as fruit reaches full yellow color and
  target size; the variety is bred for uniform fruit form and stable size
  across the harvest period
  ([Rijk Zwaan - SVEN F1](https://www.rijkzwaan.de/paprika-s%C3%BC%C3%9F/SVEN-F1-35-220-prdCA10805-ctgCrops.sweet-pepper)).
- Used fresh or cooked, like other blocky sweet peppers.

## Notes
- Crop rotation: avoid growing after other Solanaceae on the same spot for
  several years, as documented in the general `Paprika` note.
- `Sven` is bred primarily for commercial/professional growing (Rijk Zwaan is
  a professional vegetable-breeding company); figures such as fruit weight
  and the trial result are professional-scale data and may not translate
  one-to-one to small-scale garden yield expectations.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Growth duration, harvest window and propagation duration: no
  `Sven`-specific figures were found. The general `Paprika` note gives 70
  days (transplanting to first harvest), 90 days (harvest window) and 70 days
  (sowing to transplanting); these are intentionally reused for `Sven`, and
  no variety-specific source contradicts them. Yellow blocky types ripen
  fully through green to yellow, which fits the general value. This is a
  reuse, not a `Sven`-specific source claim.
- Spacing and sowing timing: reused from the general `Paprika` note (40 x 40
  cm, late February to early March sowing, mid-May outdoor planting). No
  `Sven`-specific source contradicts these general values, and no
  `Sven`-specific source gives its own figures either.
- Yield: the general `Paprika` note leaves yield open at the general-crop
  level. `Sven` is described qualitatively as "high yielding" and "high
  overall yields" in the breeder source and the Weihenstephan-Triesdorf trial,
  but no kg/m² or kg/plant figure was found for `Sven` specifically, so no
  yield value is set here either.
- Fruit weight (180-200 g) is `Sven`-specific and does not have a general
  crop equivalent to compare against, since the general `Paprika` note does
  not track fruit weight.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration 70 days, harvest window 90 days, propagation duration 70
  days: reused from the general `Paprika` note (`pepper-paprika.md`), where
  the source basis and derivation are documented. No `Sven` figure was found.
- Field/form constraint: whole integer days.
- Uncertainty: blocky yellow fruit may need a few weeks more than the
  earliest green harvest; the value fits ripe yellow fruit in a tunnel or a
  warm open-field site.
