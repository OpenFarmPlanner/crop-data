## Short Description
- `Green in Snow` (`Grün im Schnee`, `Gruen im Schnee`) is a mustard greens
  variety best mapped under the crop `Mustard greens` (`Blattsenf`).
- Botanical identity: `Brassica juncea var. multiceps` from
  [ReinSaat - Green in Snow](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/salate/asiasalate/gruen_im_schnee/);
  `Brassica juncea` from [Hof Jeebel - Green in Snow](https://biogartenversand.de/biosaatgut/blattgemuese/blattgemuese-gruen-im-schnee)
  and [Naturgarten-Samen - Blattsenf Green in Snow](https://naturgarten-samen.de/produkt/blattsenf-gruen-im-schnee-50-korn/).
- Growth duration: open as a variety-specific structured value; the sources
  agree that the variety is fast, but the available harvest timing depends on
  sowing season and protection.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow outdoors for leaf or rosette harvest. ReinSaat
  supports early protected outdoor sowing and late-summer to early-autumn
  outdoor sowing; Hof Jeebel and Naturgarten-Samen give broad spring-to-late
  summer outdoor ranges.
- Sowing: source ranges span February/March under protection through September,
  depending on supplier and cultivation setup.
- Sowing depth: 1-2 cm based on ReinSaat; Naturgarten-Samen gives 1 cm.

### Transplants
- Description: transplants or protected plantings can be used for autumn,
  winter, and early protected crops where infrastructure allows it.
- Sowing: source ranges include July to winter for protected follow-on crops.
- Outdoor planting: open; Hof Jeebel gives protected and outdoor planting
  periods but not one reusable transplant-age value.

- Spacing: source-backed options include broadcast sowing, row spacing around
  15-25 cm with close in-row spacing for leaf harvest, and wider 25 x 25 cm or
  30 x 30 cm spacing where rosette harvest is intended.
- Site: sunny to partially shaded; moist, nutrient-rich, well-drained soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest individual leaves, cut leaves, or whole rosettes according to spacing
  and crop stage.
- Use fresh in salads when young or as a cooked leafy vegetable; all culinary
  wording here is paraphrased from the supplier descriptions.
- Hof Jeebel gives a harvest range from March to late November. ReinSaat also
  presents the variety as suitable for autumn and winter cultivation outdoors
  or in protected cold structures.

## Notes
- Do not map this variety to the broad crop name `Asian leaf
  vegetables/mustard cabbage`. [ReinSaat - Asian greens category](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/salate/asiasalate/)
  combines `Brassica juncea` and `Brassica rapa` and lists several different
  Asian greens with different harvest forms.
- The supported crop identity for this variety is `Mustard greens`
  (`Blattsenf`), with `Green in Snow` as the variety name.
- The German umlaut spelling `Grün im Schnee` may appear after ASCII
  normalization; keep `Green in Snow` as the English note title and preserve
  the German variety name where useful.

## Comparison With General Crop Data

`Green in Snow` was compared with the general mustard greens note. The crop
identity matches the general crop because all product-level botanical sources
place the variety in `Brassica juncea`.

No reliable single variety-specific growth-duration value was found. The
general mustard greens note also leaves growth duration open because warm-season
baby-leaf harvests and winter crops differ substantially.

No reliable variety-specific yield value was found. Any future yield value
should distinguish cut-leaf harvest from rosette harvest before being reused or
derived.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Crop identity: map to `Mustard greens` (`Blattsenf`). ReinSaat's product
  page gives `Brassica juncea var. multiceps`, while Hof Jeebel and
  Naturgarten-Samen give `Brassica juncea`. The wider ReinSaat category is too
  broad because it also includes `Brassica rapa` crops.
- Sowing depth: use 1 cm if a single integer planning value is needed, because
  it is inside ReinSaat's 1-2 cm range and matches Naturgarten-Samen. Document
  it as a rounded planning value, not as a universal mustard-greens rule.
- Spacing: leave the structured value open unless the harvest form is selected.
  Close row spacing fits cut-leaf production, while wider square spacing fits
  rosette production.
- Harvest window: leave open. The source set gives calendar ranges and
  repeated-harvest logic, but not a single duration in days.
