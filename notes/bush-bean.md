## Short Description
- Bush bean (`Buschbohne`) is the dwarf form of `Phaseolus vulgaris`, grown
  here for green pods (distinct from the dry-seed `Kidneybohne` use).
- Warm-season, frost-sensitive annual legume; fixes nitrogen, so nutrient
  demand is low to moderate. Even moisture during flowering and pod set.
- Growth duration is short: about 8 weeks (50-60 days) from sowing to first
  green pods; recommended planning value 56 days.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow into warm soil (about 12-15 C) after the last frost;
  beans dislike root disturbance, so pre-cultivation is unusual.
- Sowing: about mid May to early July, in successions.
- Sowing depth: 3-4 cm.

- Spacing: about 40 cm between rows, in clusters or every few cm in the row.
- Site: full sun, warm, sheltered, well-drained soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Pick pods young and tender, repeatedly over a few weeks; regular picking
  keeps the plant producing.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- The earlier live general record carried growth duration 49 days, harvest
  window 21 days, and yield about 1.3 kg/m² (`per_sqm`). Re-verified on
  2026-09-21: the 49 days sit below every source found, so the recommended
  planning values are now growth duration 56 days and harvest window 11 days
  (see `Planning Value Derivations`). The live record still needs to be
  updated in a private sync; the yield value was not re-checked.
- The variety notes [Maxi](bush-bean-maxi.md) (63 days) and
  [Rocdor](bush-bean-rocdor.md) (60 days) deviate from the general 56 days
  and document why; both reuse the harvest window.
- Thousand kernel weight: 160 g, matching the live variety record `Faraday`
  (160.72 g); green bush-bean seed is far lighter than large kidney-bean seed.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Thousand kernel weight: 160 g as a planning value for a green bush bean.
  `Phaseolus vulgaris` seed spans about 250-530 g per thousand across all types
  ([pflanzen-deutschland](https://www.pflanzen-deutschland.de/Phaseolus_vulgaris.html)),
  but green bush types sit well below that; the project's `Faraday` record
  measures 160.72 g, so 160 g is used for the general crop.
- Growth duration: 56 days from sowing to first green pods. Source basis:
  [Pötschke](https://www.poetschke.de/buschbohnen-anbauen-aussaat-pflege-und-ernte/)
  gives about 8 weeks (56 days), a general guide summary gives 50-60 days, and
  the early variety
  [Saxa](https://www.biogartenbedarf.de/bio-saatgut/bohnen-saatgut/buschbohne-saxa/)
  needs 60-66 days. The value is inferred from these; it fits a
  mid-season green bush bean and is shorter for very early types.
- Harvest window: 11 days per sowing, the midpoint of the 8-14 days given by
  [LWG Bayern](https://www.lwg.bayern.de/gartenakademie/gartendokumente/infoschriften/064339/index.php).
  Inferred rounding; longer if plants are picked regularly and stay healthy.
- Propagation duration: left empty. Bush beans are direct sown.
