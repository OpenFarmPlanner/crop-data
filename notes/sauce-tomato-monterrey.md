## Short Description
- `Monterrey` is researched here as a San-Marzano-type sauce tomato:
  elongated, pointed-bottom "bottle" fruit shape, deep-red firm flesh, low
  acidity, held on long trusses of 7-8 fruits, per the general San-Marzano
  fruit description in the sources reviewed
  ([Plantura](https://www.plantura.garden/gemuese/tomatensorten/san-marzano-tomate)).
  This is a type note, not a separate Kultur: Roma-type and San-Marzano-type
  sauce tomatoes are both filed under `Soßentomate` by
  [ReinSaat](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/tomaten_paradeiser/); see
  [Bolstar](sauce-tomato-bolstar.md) for the Roma-type sibling variety in
  this project.
- The sources reviewed describe the San-Marzano fruit type in general (up
  to about 10 cm long, indeterminate to about 150 cm tall, up to 5 kg fruit
  per plant) rather than the `Monterrey` cultivar specifically by name; see
  the open source-conflict note below.
- Growth duration: 70 days from transplanting; harvest window 60 days;
  propagation duration 49 days. No variety-specific figure was found, so the
  general `Soßentomate` planning values are intentionally reused (see `Comparison
  With General Crop Data`).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: Sow indoors on a windowsill or under grow lights, plant out
  after the last frost, ideally in a greenhouse or against a warm,
  sheltered, sunny wall.
- Sowing: 02-15 to 03-31 (reused from the general `Soßentomate` window)
- Outdoor planting: from mid-May, after the last frost

- Spacing: 70-80 cm rows x 35-45 cm in-row is used as the decided planning
  value (see `Planning Value Derivations` for the reasoning). A second,
  professional-cultivation figure of about 100 x 33 cm (roughly 2 plants/m²)
  is also reported and is kept documented here rather than silently dropped
  ([samen.de](https://samen.de/blog/san-marzano-tomaten-anbau-fuer-perfekte-saucen.html)).
- Sowing depth: about 0.5 cm, reused from the general `Soßentomate` note; no
  `Monterrey`-specific figure was found.
- Site: greenhouse or sheltered, sunny, warm site preferred; prone to
  cracking and fungal disease if grown exposed in wet conditions

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest when fully red and firm.
- Classic paste/sauce tomato; low water content and firm, meaty flesh suit
  passata and canning.

## Notes
- Reported yield of up to 5 kg fruit per plant for the San-Marzano fruit
  type in general (see Planning Value Derivations for why this is not
  converted into a per-m² value here).

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Fruit shape and use match the general `Soßentomate` description directly.
- Spacing: both reported spacing options fall inside or near the general
  crop's 70-100 cm row / 35-60 cm in-row range; the professional 33 cm
  in-row figure is slightly tighter than the general range's 35 cm floor.
  The 70-80 cm row x 35-45 cm in-row (home-garden) option was chosen as the
  planning value rather than the 100 x 33 cm professional figure - see
  `Planning Value Derivations` for the reasoning.
- Yield: the general `Soßentomate` note now sets 6.0 kg/m² (see
  `sauce-tomato.md`). No `Monterrey`-specific figure was found; the general
  value is intentionally reused. No variety-specific source contradicts it.
- Nutrient demand: reused from the general `Soßentomate` note (high); no
  `Monterrey`-specific source was found.
- Growth duration, harvest window and propagation duration: no
  `Monterrey`-specific day counts were found. The general `Soßentomate` note gives
  70 days (transplanting to first harvest), 60 days (harvest window) and 49
  days (sowing to transplanting); these are intentionally reused, and no
  variety-specific source contradicts them. Qualitative wording about
  earliness or vigor in the sources was not converted into a number. This is a
  reuse, not a `Monterrey`-specific source claim.
- Sowing window: reused from the general crop as no variety-specific date was
  found. Yield: left open, see `Planning Value Derivations`.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration 70 days, harvest window 60 days, propagation duration
  49 days: reused from the general `Soßentomate` note, where the source basis and
  derivation are documented. No `Monterrey` day count was found.
- Field/form constraint: whole integer days.
- Uncertainty: fits a staked crop planted out in mid-May; protected
  cultivation or an earlier variety can start about four weeks earlier.
- Spacing decision (Monterrey): 70-80 cm rows x 35-45 cm in-row. Two options
  were reported: this home-garden figure, and a professional-cultivation
  figure of about 100 x 33 cm (~2 plants/m²)
  ([samen.de](https://samen.de/blog/san-marzano-tomaten-anbau-fuer-perfekte-saucen.html)).
  Reasoning: `Monterrey` is researched here as a staked, single-stem
  San-Marzano-type home/market-garden tomato (see Short Description), not as
  a mechanized or multi-leader professional system. The 100 x 33 cm figure
  reads as a high-density professional in-row spacing intended for
  intensive, often multi-stem or closely managed commercial rows, which does
  not match the staked single-stem cultivation this note otherwise describes
  (support structure, "geschützter, sonniger Standort"). The 70-80 cm row x
  35-45 cm in-row figure is therefore chosen as the better fit for a staked
  San-Marzano-type plant grown the way this note describes it; the
  professional figure remains documented above as an alternative rather than
  silently dropped.
- Yield: 6.0 kg/m², reused from the general `Soßentomate` note. Source basis:
  [Tomaten.de - San-Marzano-Tomate](https://www.tomaten.de/san-marzano-tomate/)
  gives "up to 5 kg per plant" for the San-Marzano fruit type in general, not
  for `Monterrey` specifically; this maximum figure is still not converted to
  a kg/m² value directly, since it is a stated ceiling rather than a
  reliable average, and multiplying it out would create a falsely precise
  number. Since the general `Soßentomate` note now has a reliable inferred
  planning value (6.0 kg/m², derived from a more representative 2-3 kg/plant
  average), that general value is reused for `Monterrey` instead, per the
  "Intentional General Planning Values" rule. No `Monterrey`-specific source
  contradicts it.
- Sowing depth: 0.5 cm, reused from the general `Soßentomate` note.
