## Short Description
- `Afrodite` is a curly-leaf (kraus) leaf-parsley variety, `Petroselinum
  crispum`. Typ: kraus (curly), an early type within the "Petersilie, Blatt"
  Kultur (see `leaf-parsley.md` for the Typ-vs-Kultur reasoning).
- Compact, dark green, tightly crinkled leaves with good uniformity in size
  and curl; well suited to pot and balcony-box cultivation; often used fresh
  because it loses aroma on drying
  ([Garten Schlüter](https://www.garten-schlueter.de/saatgut/kraeuter-saatgut/petersilie/petersilie-aromapetersilie-afrodite)).
- Growth duration: 97 days from direct sowing to first cut (general smooth
  value 90 plus about 7 days for curly types); harvest window 126 days as
  cutting period (first to fourth cut, about 6 weeks between cuts). See
  Planning Value Derivations.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow into a fine, moist seedbed; slow germination, as for
  leaf parsley generally. Well suited to container sowing as well as field
  rows.
- Sowing: about March/April to early September, in successions (general crop
  guidance; no `Afrodite`-specific window found).
- Sowing depth: about 0.5-1.5 cm (general crop guidance).

- Spacing: about 20 cm between rows, 8-10 cm within the row (general crop
  guidance).
- Site: sun to partial shade; humus-rich, evenly moist soil.

## Harvest & Use
- Cut leaves repeatedly once established; the compact, curly habit suits pot
  and balcony cultivation as well as field beds.
- Best used fresh; aroma diminishes on drying.

## Notes
- Some retailers spell this variety "Aphrodite" rather than "Afrodite"; this
  note follows the spelling given in the task ("Afrodite") and treats the two
  spellings as the same variety, since product descriptions and images match.
  Documented explicitly rather than silently picking one spelling as
  authoritative.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration: 97 days from direct sowing to first cut. Source basis: the
  "+DLRn+" gives 80-92 days for the smooth variety `Felicia` and states that
  curly or compact types need about 7 days more. Derivation: general value 90
  + 7 = 97. Variety-specific delta comes from a type-level statement, not from
  a `Afrodite`-specific source. Uncertainty about +/- 10 days.
- Harvest window: 126 days, inferred as the cutting period: 3 intervals x 42
  days (about 6 weeks between cuts for curly parsley, up to 4 cuts per year,
  same sheet). Sowings from May onward usually allow only 3 cuts (about 84
  days). Not a direct source value.
