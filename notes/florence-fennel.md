## Short Description
- Florence fennel / bulb fennel (`Knollenfenchel`) is `Foeniculum vulgare var.
  azoricum`, grown for the swollen leaf-base "bulb". Distinct from the herb
  and seed fennel.
- Cool-season biennial grown as an annual. Moderate nutrient demand, very even
  moisture (drought and check stress trigger bolting).
- Growth duration: about 60-80 days from planting, roughly 90-120 days from
  sowing, depending on sowing date, size wanted, and variety.

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivation is preferred for an even stand; grow seedlings
  cool and bright, do not plant too deep, and do not plant out unprotected
  before about mid May (bolting risk from cold checks).
- Sowing: successional from about early March; main sowings late spring to
  midsummer for autumn bulbs.
- Outdoor planting: about 6 weeks after sowing.

### Direct Sowing
- Description: possible into warm, evenly moist soil; thin to stand.
- Sowing depth: about 1 cm.

- Spacing: about 40 cm between rows, 25-30 cm within the row.
- Site: full sun, deep humus-rich moisture-retentive soil. Even watering and,
  once bulbs reach a few cm, earthing up support growth and blanching.

## Harvest & Use
- Cut bulbs from about fist size. Early sowings are harvested before flowering;
  late sowings can be lifted with the root from October/November and tolerate
  light frost.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- The live variety record `Lorenzo` (`RS-Fe-01.25`) is a compact white bulb
  type. This general note is created to carry general fennel facts; `Lorenzo`
  should be compared against it explicitly when its note is written.
- Growth duration: general 70 days for the field/main-crop phase, reused from
  the `Lorenzo` record, which is inside the 60-80 day from-planting range.
- Harvest window: general 14 days, reused from the `Lorenzo` record;
  [Hortipendium](https://www.hortipendium.de/Knollenfenchel_im_Hausgarten)
  gives about 14-17 days per sowing.
- Yield: general 3.0 kg/m² (`per_sqm`), reused from the `Lorenzo` record;
  consistent with a bulb around 300-400 g at roughly 8-10 plants/m².
- Propagation duration: about 42 days (pre-cultivation), reused from `Lorenzo`.
