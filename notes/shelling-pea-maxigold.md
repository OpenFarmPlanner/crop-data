## Short Description
- `Maxigold` (also listed as `Maxigolt`) is a marrowfat shelling pea
  (`Markerbse`), `Pisum sativum`, valued for very large, noticeably sweet
  seed.
- Medium-late, high-yielding, robust variety reaching about 80-100 cm in
  height; resistant to Fusarium
  ([Bobby-Seeds](https://www.bobby-seeds.com/gemuesesamen/bohnen-erbsensamen/erbsensamen/2408/erbse-maxigolt-markerbse)).
- Growth duration: about 12-20 weeks from sowing to harvest, inferred from the
  sowing and harvest windows below (see `Planning Value Derivations`); no
  direct day-count source was found.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow; as a marrowfat type, `Maxigold` benefits from
  slightly warmer soil than round-seeded `Palerbse` types at sowing. Staking
  or support is recommended for the tall, robust growth.
- Sowing: about April to June
  ([Bobby-Seeds](https://www.bobby-seeds.com/gemuesesamen/bohnen-erbsensamen/erbsensamen/2408/erbse-maxigolt-markerbse)).
- Sowing depth: reused from the general crop (about 3-5 cm); no
  `Maxigold`-specific depth figure was found.

- Spacing: about 30-40 cm between rows, 4-5 cm within the row
  ([Bobby-Seeds](https://www.bobby-seeds.com/gemuesesamen/bohnen-erbsensamen/erbsensamen/2408/erbse-maxigolt-markerbse)).
- Site: full sun; support recommended given the 80-100 cm height.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest late June through September, picking pods once seeds have filled
  out but are still tender; shell for fresh use or freezing.
