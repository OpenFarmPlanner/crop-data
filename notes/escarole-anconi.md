## Short Description
- `Anconi` (`Anconi RZ`) is a smooth, broad-leaved escarole variety
  (`Cichorium endivia var. latifolium`, German "glattblättrige Endivie"), bred
  by Rijk Zwaan for summer and autumn cultivation. It is a dark green type
  with a narrow leaf and a well-filled heart, developed with the fresh-cut
  industry in mind as well as for fresh market use.
- Upright growth with a healthy leaf underside speeds up harvest and
  processing; good head filling gives a comparatively high harvest weight. The
  breeder describes it as very resistant to tipburn and slow to bolt.
- Growth duration: 70 days from transplanting, reused from the general crop
  note `escarole.md` (see `Comparison With General Crop Data`).
- Harvest window: 21 days and propagation duration: 25 days, both reused from
  the general crop.

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: raise transplants and plant out for summer and autumn harvest,
  following the general escarole windows; the variety source gives no sowing
  calendar beyond "summer and autumn cultivation".
- Sowing: 2026-06-01 to 2026-07-20 (general escarole window; no variety-specific
  dates found).
- Outdoor planting: 3-4 weeks after sowing (general escarole value).
- Sowing depth: 1 cm (general escarole value; no variety-specific figure
  found).

- Spacing: about 35 cm between rows; 30 cm within the row (general escarole
  value; no variety-specific spacing found).
- Site: sun to partial shade, evenly moist soil with balanced nutrition
  (general escarole guidance; no variety-specific site requirement found).

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest once heads are well filled; the upright habit and clean leaf
  underside are described as easing harvest and processing, so the variety is
  marketed for the fresh-cut industry and for fresh-market sale.

## Notes

  it as a smooth, dark green endive, so it is an escarole (smooth) type, not a
  curly frisée type. Confidence: high for the type; medium for the wording
