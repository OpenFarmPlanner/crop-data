## Short Description
- Savoy cabbage (`Wirsing`) is the crinkled-leaf heading cabbage
  `Brassica oleracea var. sabauda`, used for autumn and winter harvest; winter
  types tolerate frost.
- Biennial grown as an annual. High nutrient demand, steady moisture, 3-4 year
  brassica rotation break.
- Growth duration ranges widely: early summer types about 60-90 days from
  sowing, autumn and winter types about 120-140 days, some standing in the
  field into early spring.

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate and transplant. Autumn and winter savoy is the
  main use here.
- Sowing: about May to July for a November-to-March harvest.
- Outdoor planting: about 5-6 weeks after sowing.

- Spacing: about 50 cm between rows and within the row.
- Site: full sun, nutrient-rich soil, firm seedbed. Net cover against cabbage
  fly and caterpillars.

## Harvest & Use
- Cut heads from autumn onward as they firm up. Winter types are harvested
  gradually through the cold season and are frost-hardy.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration: 95 days as an inferred planning value for an
  autumn/winter savoy from a spring-to-early-summer sowing. Garden sources give
  about 60-140 days depending on type
  ([meine-ernte](https://www.meine-ernte.de/pflanzen-a-z/gemuese/wirsing/),
  [Plantura](https://www.plantura.garden/gemuese/wirsing/wirsing-pflanzen)),
  with a common "3-4 months after planting" rule of thumb. 95 days is a
  whole-day mid value; early types are faster, hard winter types slower.
  Documented as inferred. The live variety record `Vertus 3`, a late winter
  type, stores 85 days.
- Harvest window: about 60 days is kept as the stored general value; winter
  savoy is picked gradually over a long period.
- Yield: about 4.0 kg/m² is the stored general value; not re-derived here.
- Propagation duration: about 42 days (pre-cultivation), already stored.
