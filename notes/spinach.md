## Short Description
- Spinach (`Spinat`) is `Spinacia oleracea`, a fast cool-season leafy crop for
  spring and autumn/winter harvest. Bolts quickly in long warm days.
- Moderate nutrient demand; even moisture. Often direct sown in successions.
- Growth duration: about 6-8 weeks to a leaf harvest in the main season,
  longer for late autumn sowings that overwinter.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow in rows; thin lightly. Pre-cultivation is unusual.
- Sowing: about February to April for late spring harvest, and about August to
  October for autumn, winter, and early spring harvest.
- Sowing depth: 2-3 cm.

- Spacing: about 20-25 cm between rows, a few cm within the row.
- Site: sun to light shade; cool conditions; humus-rich, moisture-retentive
  soil. Warm long days trigger bolting.

## Germination
- Optimal germination temperature: 10-20 °C; minimum: 2 °C
- Germination time: 6-15 days
- Source: [ProSpecieRara - Angaben zur Pflanzenanzucht](https://cms.prospecierara.ch/assets/website/prospecierara.ch/Pflanzen/Wissen/20221214_Angaben_zur_Pflanzenzucht_by_ProSpecieRara.pdf) (direct sowing; spring and late-summer sowings).

## Companion Planting
Evidence: unless stated otherwise, every entry comes from the
[BUND - Mischkultur: Gute und schlechte Nachbarn](https://www.bund.net/fileadmin/user_upload_bund/publikationen/naturnahes-gaertnern/Mischkultur-Gemuese-BUND.pdf) neighbour table, a list of gardening experience without reasons or
trial data, so the reason is "other" throughout. The
[Bayerische Gartenakademie - Mischkulturen im Gemüsegarten, Merkblatt 2104](https://www.lwg.bayern.de/mam/cms06/gartenakademie/dateien/2104-2020.pdf) states that neighbour effects are hard to prove and that reported
observations often contradict each other, because site and care matter as
well. Treat these entries as gardening tradition. "Both rows" means the
table lists the pair under each of the two crops; "one row" means only under
the crop named.

Good neighbours:
- Celeriac (`Sellerie`): other; both rows.
- Potato (`Kartoffel`): other; both rows.
- Tomato (`Tomate`): other; both rows.
- Pole bean (`Stangenbohne`): other; both rows.
- Kohlrabi: other; both rows.
- Radish (`Radieschen`, `Rettich`): other; both rows.
- Rhubarb (`Rhabarber`): other; both rows.
- Strawberry (`Erdbeere`): other; both rows.
- Sweet corn (`Mais`): other (use of free space). The
  [Bayerische Gartenakademie - Mischkulturen im Gemüsegarten, Merkblatt 2104](https://www.lwg.bayern.de/mam/cms06/gartenakademie/dateien/2104-2020.pdf) sows spinach between young sweet-corn rows from early March
  and harvests it by mid-May; spinach left too long stunts the corn.
  Extension example, not in the BUND table.
- Cabbage crops (`Kohlgewächse`): other; both rows. Mapping open.

Bad neighbours:
- Beetroot (`Rote Bete`): other; spinach row only.
- Swiss chard (`Mangold`): other; spinach row only. All three crops are
  Amaranthaceae; the source gives no reason, so a shared-pest reason is
  not assumed.

## Harvest & Use
- Cut whole young plants or pick outer leaves. Overwintered stands are cut in
  early spring.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration: 50 days as an inferred planning value for a main-season leaf
  harvest from direct sowing. Garden sources give about 10-12 weeks to a full
  plant but note a usable leaf harvest after roughly 6-8 weeks
  ([meine-ernte](https://www.meine-ernte.de/pflanzen-a-z/gemuese/spinat/),
  [Plantura](https://www.plantura.garden/gemuese/spinat/spinat-anbauen)).
  50 days sits at the leaf-harvest end and is documented as inferred. The live
  variety record `Matador` stores 49 days. Overwintering sowings take much
  longer in calendar terms.
- Harvest window: 21 days as a planning value; a spinach stand is usable for
  only a short period before bolting or quality loss. The live variety record
  `Matador` also stores 21 days.
- Yield: about 2.5 kg/m² is the stored general value; corroborated, not
  re-derived, on 2026-09-22 by independent trial figures: a mid-early spinach
  variety trial reports `Disko` and `Timor` at about 2.5 kg/m² each, and a
  [LVG Baden-Württemberg organic trial](https://lvg.landwirtschaft-bw.de/site/pbs-bw-mlr-root/get/documents_E-680384267/MLR.LEL/PB5Documents/lvg/pdf/2/2012%20%C3%96ko%20Versuchsbericht%20Spinat%20Satz%201.pdf?attachment=true)
  reports `Ranchero` and `Racoon` at about 2.5 kg/m² each. 2.5 kg/m² is
  therefore kept as a source-consistent, if still garden/trial-summary-level,
  general value.
- Nutrient demand: moderate (medium), confirmed by [BUND Region Hannover -
  Stark-, Mittel- und Schwachzehrer im Gemüse- und Kräutergarten](https://bund-region-hannover.de/fileadmin/hannover/BUND_aktiv/Universum_Kleingarten/Publikationen_zum_Download/Handreichung_Stark-_Mittel-_und_Schwachzehrer.pdf),
  which lists "Spinat" as a Mittelzehrer.
- Thousand kernel weight: 12 g as a planning value. Spinach seed is commonly
  about 50-80 seeds per gram, i.e. roughly 12-20 g per thousand
  ([Osborne Seed - Seed Count Chart](https://www.osborneseed.com/pages/seed-count-chart)).
  12 g is at the lower, small-seeded end; round-seeded types can be heavier.
- Propagation duration: left empty. Spinach is direct sown.
