## Short Description
- `Anconi` is a broad-leaved (`Eskariol`/escarole-type) endive variety
  (`Cichorium endivia`), bred for summer and autumn cultivation.
- Upright growth with a healthy leaf underside is bred to speed up harvest
  and processing; good head filling gives a comparatively high harvest
  weight. The variety is described as strongly resistant to bolting and to
  tipburn.
- Growth duration: 75 days from transplanting, reused from the general crop
  (see `Comparison With General Crop Data`).
- Harvest window: 21 days and propagation duration 25 days, both reused from
  the general crop.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow or transplant for summer/autumn harvest, following
  the general crop's seasonal windows; the variety source does not give its
  own sowing calendar beyond "summer and autumn cultivation".
- Sowing: about March/April for a summer crop, mid-June to end of July for
  an autumn crop (general crop windows; no variety-specific dates found).
- Sowing depth: 1 cm (general crop value; no variety-specific figure found).

- Spacing: about 30 cm between rows, 25-30 cm within the row (general crop
  value; no variety-specific spacing found).
- Site: full sun, humus- and nutrient-rich soil (general crop guidance; no
  variety-specific site requirement found).

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest once heads are well filled; the upright habit and clean leaf
  underside are described as easing mechanical/fresh-cut harvest, so the
  variety is marketed for both fresh-cut processing and fresh-market bunches.

## Notes
