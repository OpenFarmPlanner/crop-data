## Short Description
- `Anconi` is a broad-leaved (`Eskariol`/escarole-type) endive variety
  (`Cichorium endivia`), bred for summer and autumn cultivation.
- Upright growth with a healthy leaf underside is bred to speed up harvest
  and processing; good head filling gives a comparatively high harvest
  weight. The variety is described as strongly resistant to bolting and to
  tipburn.
- Growth duration: left open; reused general uncertainty from the crop note
  applies (see `Comparison With General Crop Data`).

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest once heads are well filled; the upright habit and clean leaf
  underside are described as easing mechanical/fresh-cut harvest, so the
  variety is marketed for both fresh-cut processing and fresh-market bunches.
