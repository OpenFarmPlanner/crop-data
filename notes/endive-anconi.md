## Short Description
- `Anconi` (`Anconi RZ`) is a smooth, broad-leaved endive variety (leaf type
  `Eskariol`/Escariol, `Cichorium endivia var. latifolium`, German
  "glattblättrige Endivie"), bred by Rijk Zwaan for summer and autumn
  cultivation. It is a dark green type with a narrow leaf and a well-filled
  heart, developed with the fresh-cut industry in mind as well as for fresh
  market use.
- Upright growth with a healthy leaf underside speeds up harvest and
  processing; good head filling gives a comparatively high harvest weight. The
  breeder describes it as very resistant to tipburn and slow to bolt.
- Growth duration: 60 days from transplanting, reused from the merged general
  crop note `endive.md` (see "Comparison With General Crop Data"; this
  changed from the smooth-leaf-type-specific 70 days used before the
  2026-09-28 Kultur merge).
- Harvest window: 18 days and propagation duration: 27 days, both reused from
  the general crop.

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: raise transplants and plant out for summer and autumn harvest,
  following the general Endivie windows; the variety source gives no sowing
  calendar beyond "summer and autumn cultivation".
- Sowing: 2026-05-16 to 2026-07-17 (general Endivie window; no variety-specific
  dates found).
- Outdoor planting: 3-5 weeks after sowing (general Endivie value).
- Sowing depth: 1 cm (general Endivie value; no variety-specific figure
  found).

- Spacing: about 33 cm between rows; 30 cm within the row (general Endivie
  value; no variety-specific spacing found).
- Site: sun to partial shade, evenly moist soil with balanced nutrition
  (general Endivie guidance; no variety-specific site requirement found).

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest once heads are well filled; the upright habit and clean leaf
  underside are described as easing harvest and processing, so the variety is
  marketed for the fresh-cut industry and for fresh-market sale.

## Notes
- Type evidence: Rijk Zwaan's own product pages carry the titles
  "Endiviensamen | glattblättrig | ANCONI" and "Smooth endive", and describe
  it as a smooth, dark green endive, so it is a smooth/Eskariol type, not a
  curly Frisée type. Confidence: high for the type; medium for the wording
  of the details, because the Rijk Zwaan pages
  ([German](https://www.rijkzwaan.de/endivie/ANCONI-11-602-prdCE10018-ctgCrops.endive),
  [UK](https://www.rijkzwaan.co.uk/endive/ANCONI-RZ-prdCE10018-ctgCrops.endive))
  returned HTTP 403 for a direct read on 2026-09-24, so the description is
  taken from search-result excerpts (titles and description text), not from a
  full page read.
- [ReinSaat](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/salate/endiviensalat/) does not
  list Anconi in its endive category (checked 2026-09-24), so ReinSaat gives
  no evidence for or against the type; it is a professional variety of the
  breeder, not a typical organic-shop variety.
- Bolting and tipburn resistance are variety traits without a general crop
  equivalent; documented as notes only, not as structured values.
- The breeder claims "good head filling gives a comparatively high harvest
  weight" for this variety. Since the general crop's yield value is now a
  cross-leaf-type average (4.75 kg/m², see `endive.md`) rather than a
  smooth-type-specific figure, this variety's actual yield is plausibly above
  the general average precisely because it is a smooth type and a
  higher-filling variety within that type. This is not quantified or turned
  into a variety-specific number; flagged here so the reused general value is
  not misread as a variety-specific claim in either direction.
