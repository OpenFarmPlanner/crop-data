## Short Description
- `Kürbis` here covers edible pumpkins/squash grown for the kitchen, as
  distinct from `Zierkürbis` (ornamental pumpkin, see `notes/ornamental-
  pumpkin.md`), which is grown for decoration/carving and not primarily for
  eating.
- **Identity note:** despite ReinSaat filing both `Hokkaido` and `Butternut`
  under one shop category, "Edible Pumpkins/Squash"
  ([ReinSaat - Speisekürbis](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/)),
  the two are botanically different species: `Hokkaido` is `Cucurbita
  maxima`, while `Butternut` is `Cucurbita moschata`
  ([ReinSaat - Hokkaido Orange](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/hokkaido_orange/),
  [ReinSaat - Butternut Waltham](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/butternut_waltham/)).
  This general `Kürbis` note is kept as one Kultur (matching the shop-category
  and common garden-planning grouping), but the species difference is
  documented explicitly here rather than silently assumed away; see
  `Comparison With General Crop Data` in each variety note for how this
  affects cold-sensitivity and timing.
- Warm-season, frost-sensitive, space-demanding vining crop; both `C. maxima`
  and `C. moschata` share broadly similar cultivation needs (warm soil, full
  sun, high nutrient and water demand, large spacing), which is why this note
  is not split by species.
- Growth duration and harvest window are left open at the general-crop level:
  fruit size, storage type, and days-to-maturity vary substantially between
  `Hokkaido`-type and `Butternut`-type pumpkins, and a single figure would be
  falsely precise. See variety notes for concrete values.

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate in pots (at least 7x7 cm) on a warm windowsill or
  in a warm greenhouse at 20-22 °C.
- Sowing: mid-April to mid-May
  ([Lubera - Hokkaido pflanzen](https://www.lubera.com/at/gartenbuch/hokkaido-pflanzen-p3814)).
- Outdoor planting: from mid-May, after the last frosts, once soil temperature
  is at least 10-12 °C
  ([ReinSaat - Hokkaido Orange](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/hokkaido_orange/)).

### Direct Sowing
- Description: sow 2 seeds per hole directly once frost risk has passed and
  soil has warmed sufficiently.
- Sowing: mid-May onward, once soil temperature reaches at least 10-15 °C and
  no more night frosts are expected
  ([onfarming.at](https://www.onfarming.at/inhalt/sortiment-ratgeber/ratgeber/landwirte/ackerbau/aussaat/kurbis-sorten-anbau-und-kulturfuhrung)).
- Sowing depth: 2-3 cm
  ([various sources, consistent figure](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/EN/kuerbis/speisekuerbis/hokkaido_orange/)).

- Spacing: general garden guidance is about 100-150 cm both between and
  within rows due to the crop's large space demand; commercial guidance gives
  a wider range of 1.5-2.5 m between rows and 0.5-1.5 m within the row
  depending on variety vigor
  ([onfarming.at](https://www.onfarming.at/inhalt/sortiment-ratgeber/ratgeber/landwirte/ackerbau/aussaat/kurbis-sorten-anbau-und-kulturfuhrung)).
  Variety notes give more specific figures where available.
- Site: full sun, warm, wind-sheltered; nutrient-rich, well-drained soil with
  high water demand during fruit development.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest from late summer into autumn once fruits are fully ripe (skin
  hardened, stem corked); most types store well after full ripening and
  curing.
- Used fresh-cooked; flesh characteristics vary strongly by type/species (see
  variety notes).

## Notes
- Crop rotation: avoid growing after other Cucurbitaceae (cucumber, squash,
  zucchini, melon) on the same spot for several years.
- Nutrient demand is high: one commercial source gives roughly 100 kg N,
  85 kg P, and 225 kg K per hectare for a yield of about 400 dt/ha
  ([onfarming.at](https://www.onfarming.at/inhalt/sortiment-ratgeber/ratgeber/landwirte/ackerbau/aussaat/kurbis-sorten-anbau-und-kulturfuhrung)).
- General yield range across pumpkin/squash types: about 5-15 kg/m² in
  commercial field cultivation
  ([onfarming.at](https://www.onfarming.at/inhalt/sortiment-ratgeber/ratgeber/landwirte/ackerbau/aussaat/kurbis-sorten-anbau-und-kulturfuhrung)).
  This wide range is not narrowed to a single general-crop value because
  fruit size and plant vigor differ strongly between types; variety notes
  should give more specific values where available.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Yield: 5-15 kg/m² is a wide commercial-field range covering many pumpkin
  and squash types of different fruit size; not narrowed to one general-crop
  figure since `Hokkaido` (small, ~2-3 kg fruit) and `Butternut` (larger,
  up to 3.5 kg fruit, different plant habit) diverge enough that a single
  number would be falsely precise. Variety notes carry more specific figures
  where sourced.
- Growth duration / harvest window: left open at the general-crop level for
  the same reason; see variety notes.
