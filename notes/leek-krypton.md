## Short Description
- `Krypton F1` is an early/summer-type leek with a semi-long shaft, easy to
  peel, with healthy, straight, dark green foliage and good bunching quality
  ([VOLTZ - Porree/Lauch Krypton F1](https://de.de-shop.voltz-maraichage.com/bio-saatgut/porree-lauch-krypton-f1)).
- In a Nunhems varietal comparison, `Krypton` scores high for earliness
  (8/10) and plant type (8/10), with a moderate shaft-length score (6/10) and
  leaf color score (7/10) relative to other leek varieties in the same
  catalog ([Nunhems - Porree Sortiment](https://www.yumpu.com/de/document/view/18962169/porree-sortiment-nunhems)).
- Growth duration: sources agree this is an early/summer type harvested from
  July, but no fully verifiable exact day count was found; see `Research
  Status`. A commercial planting-window listing places transplanting around
  calendar weeks 21-27 (late May to early July)
  ([Meijers Planten - Krypton](https://www.meijersplanten.nl/de/porreepflanzen/krypton/76)).

## Sowing & Planting

### Transplants
- Description: pre-cultivate indoors, then transplant for an early/summer
  harvest, consistent with the general `Porree` crop note's early-type
  timing.
- Sowing: January (general early-type window; no `Krypton`-specific sowing
  date beyond this was found).
- Outdoor planting: late March to end of April, consistent with the general
  crop note; a separate commercial listing gives transplanting weeks 21-27
  (late May to early July), which may reflect a later succession slot rather
  than the earliest possible planting.

- Spacing: no `Krypton`-specific figure found; see general crop note (about
  15 cm within the row, 50 cm between rows).
- Site: no `Krypton`-specific site guidance found; see general crop note.

## Harvest & Use
- Harvested mid-July through August as an early/summer type, per the general
  crop note's early-type harvest window; no `Krypton`-specific end date was
  found.
- Semi-long shaft, easy to peel; suited to fresh-market bunching.

## Notes
- A search-engine-summarized agricultural trial document (a PDF hosted at
  `media.diemayrei.de`) suggested a roughly 95-day growth cycle and a yield
  figure of about 378 dt/ha in a 2020 trial, but the document could not be
  read directly to verify these numbers against their original context (crop,
  units, trial conditions). These figures are therefore not used as
  planning values and are flagged here only as an unverified lead for future
  research, per the "Explicit, Not Silent" rule.
