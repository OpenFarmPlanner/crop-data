## Short Description
- `Bambergerhörnchen` (also written `Bamberger Hörnchen` / `Bamberger
  Hörnla`) is a small, fingerling-type heritage potato (`Solanum tuberosum`):
  finger-sized, slightly curved tubers with a thin, pale ochre skin showing a
  faint reddish shimmer, and dense, firm, deep-yellow flesh with a nutty
  flavor
  ([Utopia - Bamberger Hörnchen](https://utopia.de/ratgeber/bamberger-hoernchen-anbau-ernte-und-wissenswertes-zur-kartoffelsorte_162984/)).
- Small-growing plant with thin, delicate foliage and white blossoms; firm
  cooking type ("festkochend")
  ([Utopia - Bamberger Hörnchen](https://utopia.de/ratgeber/bamberger-hoernchen-anbau-ernte-und-wissenswertes-zur-kartoffelsorte_162984/)).
- Maturity group: sources conflict. One source describes it as a late
  variety, cultivated from April until the foliage dies back in October; a
  different source instead gives a 120-140 day vegetation period, which
  matches the "mid-early" range from the general `Kartoffel` note rather than
  "late". This conflict is documented explicitly rather than silently
  resolved; see `Comparison With General Crop Data`
  ([Stadtverband Leipzig der Kleingärtner - Bamberger Hörnchen](https://www.stadtverband-leipzig.de/pflanzenraritaeten-bamberger-hoernchen/),
  [derkleinegarten.de - Bamberger Hörnchen](https://www.derkleinegarten.de/nutzgarten-kleingarten/gemuesegarten-anlegen/kartoffelanbau/speisekartoffeln-sorten/bamberger-hoernchen.html)).
- Yield: described as noticeably below-average, only about 20-30% of a
  typical potato harvest, due to susceptibility to pests and disease and a
  generally low-yielding heritage habit; the variety is grown mainly as a
  gourmet/specialty crop despite the high care effort
  ([Utopia - Bamberger Hörnchen](https://utopia.de/ratgeber/bamberger-hoernchen-anbau-ernte-und-wissenswertes-zur-kartoffelsorte_162984/)).

## Sowing & Planting

### Seed-Tuber Planting
- Description: plant seed tubers like other potatoes; the variety is
  described as more disease- and pest-prone than average, so site rotation
  and vigilance are especially relevant.
- Planting: mid-April to mid-May
  ([Stadtverband Leipzig der Kleingärtner - Bamberger Hörnchen](https://www.stadtverband-leipzig.de/pflanzenraritaeten-bamberger-hoernchen/)).
- Planting depth: 10-15 cm is the commonly cited figure for this variety; one
  source instead describes a shallow April planting with only about 1 cm of
  soil cover, which appears to describe an early, shallow-covering technique
  rather than the final planting depth after hilling. This inconsistency is
  documented rather than silently resolved
  ([Stadtverband Leipzig der Kleingärtner - Bamberger Hörnchen](https://www.stadtverband-leipzig.de/pflanzenraritaeten-bamberger-hoernchen/)).

- Spacing: about 30-35 cm within the row, 60 cm between rows
  ([Stadtverband Leipzig der Kleingärtner - Bamberger Hörnchen](https://www.stadtverband-leipzig.de/pflanzenraritaeten-bamberger-hoernchen/)).
- Site: full sun, loose, well-drained, nutrient-rich soil, matching the
  general `Kartoffel` crop's requirements.

## Harvest & Use
- Cultivation runs from April until the foliage dies back in October,
  consistent with a late-season harvest
  ([Stadtverband Leipzig der Kleingärtner - Bamberger Hörnchen](https://www.stadtverband-leipzig.de/pflanzenraritaeten-bamberger-hoernchen/)).
- Used as a firm-cooking gourmet potato; well suited for salads and dishes
  where the nutty flavor and firm texture are showcased.

## Notes
- Crop rotation: avoid growing after other Solanaceae on the same spot for
  several years, as documented in the general `Kartoffel` note; especially
  relevant here given the variety's higher susceptibility to pests and
  disease.
- Labor-intensive and low-yielding compared with modern varieties; documented
  by multiple sources as a "Liebhaberprodukt" (enthusiast's crop) rather than
  a high-yield production variety.

## Comparison With General Crop Data
- Maturity group / growth duration: this is the key variety-specific
  difference to document. Sources conflict on whether `Bambergerhörnchen` is
  "late" (full April-to-October cultivation) or "mid-early" (120-140 days,
  per the vegetation-period figure). The general `Kartoffel` note leaves
  growth duration open specifically because maturity groups differ this
  strongly; this variety's own sources already show the same kind of
  disagreement, so no single day-count value is asserted here. Both source
  readings are documented rather than silently picking one.
- Planting depth: 10-15 cm is within the general crop's cited 6-10/10-15 cm
  conflict range; not a new deviation, but confirms the deeper end of the
  general crop's disputed range for this variety.
- Spacing: 30-35 cm in-row / 60 cm between rows is close to, but at the lower
  end of, the general crop's 30-40 cm / 60-80 cm range; treated as consistent
  rather than a meaningful deviation.
- Yield: clearly below the general crop's already-open 20-30 kg/m² garden
  guidance (only 20-30% of a typical harvest, i.e. roughly 4-9 kg/m² if that
  general range is used as the baseline - not stated directly by any source
  as a kg/m² figure, so no yield value is set here; this rough conversion is
  shown only to illustrate the scale of the deviation, not as a planning
  value). Documented as a real variety-specific deviation (lower yield) from
  the general crop, not a reuse.
