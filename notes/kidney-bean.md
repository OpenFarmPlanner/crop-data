## Short Description
- Kidney bean (`Kidneybohne`) is the dry-seed use of the bush bean
  `Phaseolus vulgaris`, grown to full pod dryness rather than for green pods.
- Warm-season, frost-sensitive annual. High water need during flowering and pod
  fill; moderate nutrient demand (legume, fixes nitrogen).
- Growth duration: longer than green-bean use because pods must ripen and dry
  on the plant.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow after the last frost into warm soil (about 15 C).
  Pre-cultivation is unusual; beans dislike root disturbance.
- Sowing: about mid May to mid June.
- Sowing depth: 3-4 cm.

- Spacing: about 40 cm between rows, 5-8 cm within the row, or in clusters.
- Site: full sun, warm, well-drained soil, sheltered from wind.

## Harvest & Use
- Leave pods on the plant until they are brown and dry, then thresh out the
  seeds and dry them further airy and cool.
- Harvest is a short concentrated window in late summer to autumn once the crop
  matures.

## Planning Value Derivations
- Growth duration: 100 days as an inferred planning value for dry-seed harvest
  from a spring sowing. General garden sources give roughly 8-10 weeks to green
  pods and about four months to a dry harvest
  ([Der kleine Garten](https://www.derkleinegarten.de/nutzgarten-kleingarten/gemuesegarten-anlegen/huelsenfruechte/kidneybohnen-anbauen.html),
  [Plantura](https://www.plantura.garden/gemuese/bohnen/bohnen-ernten)).
  100 days is a whole-day value between the ~90-day fast dry types and the
  ~120-day slow ones; documented as inferred, not a direct source value.
  The live variety record `Canadian Wonder` stores 110 days, a late type.
- Harvest window: about 60 days is kept as the general planning value (already
  stored). Dry beans mature over a few weeks; 60 days covers an uneven stand.
- Yield: about 0.23 kg/m² dry seed is the stored general value; dry-bean yields
  are far lower than green-bean yields. Not re-derived here.
- Thousand kernel weight: 470 g as a planning value for a large red-seeded
  garden bean. `Phaseolus vulgaris` seed ranges about 250-530 g per thousand
  ([pflanzen-deutschland](https://www.pflanzen-deutschland.de/Phaseolus_vulgaris.html));
  kidney types sit at the upper end, and the live variety record
  `Canadian Wonder` measures 472 g. 470 g is chosen to match.
- Propagation duration: left empty. Kidney beans are direct sown.
