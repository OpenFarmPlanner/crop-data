## Short Description
- `Sora` is a round red radish variety with firm, tender flesh and a long usable
  harvest period.
- Site: sunny to partially shaded, humus-rich, evenly moist soil; avoid fresh
  organic fertilization.
- Growth duration: 30 days to first harvest as a variety-specific planning
  value.

## Sowing & Planting

### Direct Sowing
- Description: direct sow in repeated batches for a continuous harvest.
- Sowing: 2026-01-01 to 2026-09-30
- Sowing depth: 1 cm

### Overwintering Crop
- Description: protected sowings can be used for winter or early spring
  harvest where local climate and infrastructure allow it.
- Sowing: 2026-09-15 to 2026-10-15
- Move to cold frame/greenhouse: not applicable for direct protected sowing
- Harvest: late autumn to early spring, depending on sowing date and protection

- Spacing: 12 cm between rows; 5 cm within the row
- Site: sunny to partially shaded, evenly moist, fine seedbed

## Harvest & Use
- Harvest when roots are round and marketable. Do not reduce the harvest window
  to only a few days, because multiple sources describe `Sora` as staying firm
  and tender for longer than many radishes.
- Use fresh as raw vegetables, in salads, or on bread.

## Notes
- Keep evenly moist to reduce bolting, pithiness, and excessive pungency.
- As a brassica crop, avoid short rotations after cabbage, mustard, radish, or
  other Brassicaceae.

## Comparison With General Crop Data

`Sora` was compared with the general radish crop note. The 30-day growth
duration is supported independently by a variety-specific source and only
happens to match the general radish planning value.

No reliable variety-specific single value was found for the harvest window. The
general 21-day radish planning value is intentionally reused because the crop
calendar needs a variety harvest window and the sources describe `Sora` as
remaining firm and tender for an extended period.

No reliable `Sora`-specific kg/m² yield source was found. The general radish
yield planning value can be intentionally reused if the live crop model needs a
yield value.

## Planning Value Derivations

- Growth duration: 30 days. [Hof Jeebel - Radies Sora Saatband](https://biogartenversand.de/biosaatgut/wurzelgemuese/radies-sora-saatband)
  gives first harvest after about 30 days. The value is used as a rounded
  variety-specific planning value.
- Harvest window: 21 days. No variety-specific single value was found.
  [ReinSaat - Sora](https://www.reinsaat.at/shop/DE/radies_rettich/radies/sora/)
  describes `Sora` as staying firm and tender for a long time. The value should
  be treated as an intentional general radish planning value, not as a direct
  `Sora` source claim.
- Spacing: sources range roughly from 10-25 cm between rows and 2-8 cm within
  the row. The 12 x 5 cm planning value sits in the common compact range.
